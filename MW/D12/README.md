# D12

Standard product tree — populated by the defs contract flow (S28, 2026-09-11).

The definitions are NOT here. `d12.csv` and `d12-mx-master.csv` live in the
`defs` submodule (`defs/products/d12/`, `defs/gen/matrix/`); this tree
carries only what is generated from them.

- `MX/_matrix.csv` — GENERATED by `./sync-defs.sh`
  (`defs/tools/expand_matrix.py`), hashed in `defs.lock` and watched by
  `./check-contract-drift.sh`. Do not hand-edit.

**There is no `DSP/` here, and that is the point.** Decision D3: ONE DSP4
firmware serves the range, and a product is three config words —
`CFG_PRODUCT_ID`, `CFG_CHAN_MASK`, `CFG_AUX_MASK`. D12's cells are a
STRICT SUBSET of D24's and of D32's and every one of them reaches the address
the shared map already gives it, so D12 has no address map, no node graph and
no firmware of its own. Its proposed `dsp.csv` — the intersection of the
superset graph with this product's cell set — is written to
`proposals/defs/products/d12/` by `MW/D32/DSP/gen_dsp.py --propose` for the
hub to land.

Fit, by construction and measured driven on the part:
`MW/D32/DSP/dsp4-s28-20260911.md` and `MW/D32/DSP/fit-table.csv`.
