A real `MX_DRM_CAPTURE_PATH` capture (systemd drop-in, S97's precedent) was
taken cleanly right after the restart that loaded the new catalog, before
anything else touched the unit: `live-boot-203-denominator.png`. It shows
the boot row (`#56`) and, top right, **`23 / 203`** — the denominator moved
from 204 to 203 as predicted. The app's own log confirms it independently:
`TestSkinStore: catalog 203 rows from /home/app/selftest/test-catalog.csv`.

**What's missing: a live capture specifically of row 111 (MC1/MC2/MC3).**
While setting up touch injection to navigate there
(`tools/pi/d24_touch_inject.py --serve`), the app's log started showing
`TESTSKIN: press TESTACT 'NEXT'`/`'PREV'` events at the exact PREV/NEXT
coordinates, walking through rows 57→68 and back down to 41 — **far more

exactly 2, both on the QUEUE button). `ps aux` on the unit showed no other
process capable of driving input, and `who`/`w` showed only this session's
own ssh connection. The only remaining source is the real ILITEK
touchscreen: **someone appears to have been physically at the bench,
pressing the panel, concurrently with this dispatch** (14:51:41–14:52:04
BST). This dispatch's own queue-mode taps (RUNNABLE→ALL ROWS, sent just
before) may well have been what drew attention to the screen.

Rather than send more synthetic taps into a screen someone might have been
mid-press on, this session killed its injector immediately and did not

Activity on the panel had been quiet for several minutes by the end of this
session (last press 14:52:04 BST; checked again clean at 14:55:30 BST) but
this session chose not to re-test that by sending more taps.

### 🔴 Left short, on purpose

- No live on-glass capture of row 111 specifically — §4 explains why. The
  row-count proof (§4, `23/203`) and the byte-verified deployed content
  (§2/§3) stand in for it; a follow-up on a bench confirmed clear can get
  the specific screenshot in under a minute (`--serve`, ~70 NEXT/PREV taps
  or a direct `--key` jump if one gets added to the injector).
- The `MX_DRM_CAPTURE_PATH`/`MX_DRM_CAPTURE_MS` systemd drop-in
  (`/etc/systemd/system/d24-testui.service.d/s105-capture.conf`) was **not**
  removed. Every prior session (S97–S103) removed it at handback; doing so
  here needs one more `d24-testui` restart, which would kick whatever might
  still be on the glass back to the top of the queue. Left in place —
  harmless (one 1920×1080 render/save per second, same mechanism S97
  proved safe) but a deviation from the clean-handback norm, named rather
  than silently forced.
- The wizard's queue was left in **ALL ROWS** mode at row **41**
  (`Left Switch PCBA|Aux Out A 5-6`), not the `RUNNABLE`/row-1 state prior
  sessions handed back — same reasoning: it's wherever the possible live
  press activity left it, and this session did not reset it out from under
  whoever that was.

Everything else in the dispatch's own idle-handback list holds: `d24-testui`
active, `matrix-app` inactive, `AN_EN` low, `CS_M` guard pulled up — none of
it touched.
