# S40 — the morning sheet, 2026-09-14

**The unit was never touched to write this.** S43 was a desk session; so was
S42 before it. Everything below is the cold start from the S39 write-up §10
with S42's changes folded in, in the order it is run, with the pass criterion
beside each step. Nothing here has been executed on MW-D24-2.

**Read once before starting: §0.** The analog board is mid-rework and the
analog half of S40 may not be available at all. The sheet is arranged so that
everything provable *up to the XLR* comes first and does not depend on it.

---

## 0. The unit's state to expect, and the one thing that may block half of this

PW took the analog board into rework after S39 and before S42. The hub records
three items and no more detail than this:

* **Q8 dead**
* **EN pull-downs**
* **U11 revert**

**This sheet cannot say what state that work is in, and neither can the repo.**
So the first question of the morning is PW's, not the bench's: *is the analog
board on the unit, and are its rails good?* The answer splits the session:

| analog board | what runs | what does not |
|---|---|---|
| on and good | §1–§6 then §7 (the loop) then S40 gates 3–4 | — |
| off, or rails not signed off | **§1–§6 in full** — the whole DSP side, both capacity numbers, and the TX proof to the DAC lane | §7 and S40 gates 3–4; the loop needs the converters and the output stage |

Everything in §1–§6 is a DSP-side reading and none of it needs the analog
board, the mic pre, the 595 chain or a cable. **If the analog board is out, S40
is not blocked — it is half-length**, and the half it keeps is the half that
decides whether the capture fix worked.

The rest of the unit is as S41 left it (S41's block): the `s41_mhrx_pullup_off`
bitstream on the part, `matrix-app` active, GPIO27 hi. **AN_EN (GPIO26) is read,
never written**, at every gate — and after S41's uncommanded power cycles,
read it and write down what it says rather than assuming S39's `hi`.

## 5. **The gate. `dsp4_s42_align.py` — the first real command of the session**

```bash
sudo pinctrl set 6,24 op dh; python3 dsp4_s42_align.py /home/app/s42
```

It writes nothing and moves no control. **Until it passes, no level, gain law,
meter reading or percentage on this unit means anything.**

### 5.1 RX — all twelve chip-1 converter lanes

| test | pass | fail means |
|---|---|---|
| **bit 31** | **exercised** across the twelve lanes (a quiet lane crosses zero) | never set on any word of any lane = still S39-4: **the MFD change did not reach the part. STOP.** |
| **bits 6:0** | **never set** — the 24-in-32 pad, now in the right place | set = the window moved the other way; MFD is 3 or the strap reading is wrong |
| **rms per lane**, read as Q1.31 | **−65 … −85 dBFS** | outside that band = not a converter noise floor |
| **the twelve rms values** | **distinct** (S39 measured 0.000054 … 0.000593 under the corrected reading) | twelve equal numbers = one stuck source, whatever the bit tests say |

The distinctness test is the one a stuck lane cannot fake. Record all twelve
rms figures in dBFS, per lane, in the write-up — that table is the per-lane
MFD proof and it is the evidence S40 gate 1 asks for.

### 5.2 Chip 2

Same two bit tests on chip 2's receive lanes. Chip 2's converter-side MFD is
2 and its fabric lanes are 1; the inter-chip lanes from chip 1 are fabric, so
**a chip-2 lane failing the bit tests while chip 1 passes points at the
per-lane table, not at the strap** — the table is
`c2_ic_lanes_mfd[3] = {1,1,1}` / `c2_tx_lanes_mfd[5] = {2,2,2,1,2}`.

### 5.3 TX — chip 2, resolved by name

The tool resolves `C2_AUX_OUT_01`, `C2_AUX_OUT_02`, `C2_MAIN_OUT_01`,
`C2_MAIN_OUT_02` and `C2_MAIN_ST_OUT` **by name** through `_c2_tx_ptrs` — not
by position, which was S39-5b's mistake — and prints offset, stride and peak.

**Pass: AUX 1 live and inside ±1.0 in Q4.28.** S39-6 read 1.538 (+3.75 dBFS,
above full scale); that was the RX artefact arriving at the DAC and it must
disappear with the RX fix. **MAIN Out 1/2 now live on B_O1 slots 3 and 2.**

**There is no "seven silent TX lanes" defect to chase** (S42-4): `_c2_tx_off`
is the 24-entry TX *node* table, its first eight entries are aux 1–8 carrying
nothing because nothing is routed to them, and MAIN sits at entries 12–15 and
18 — outside the window the old tool printed.

### 5.4 What the desk cannot prove

**The transmit framing is proved by the analog loop and by nothing else.**
`DSP4_LOOPBACK` is structurally blind to it: a symmetric MFD error cancels in
a wire loop by construction (S42-1). With RX right, a still-early transmit
returns a **doubled, sign-wrapped** copy — a violent artefact, not a quiet
wrong level, so it is unmistakable when §7 runs.

## 6. The capacity re-take — ten minutes, and worth more than any argument

```bash
sudo pinctrl set 6,24 op dh; python3 dsp4_s39_cost.py 1 /home/app/s42 30
sudo pinctrl set 6,24 op dh; python3 dsp4_s39_cost.py 2 /home/app/s42 30
```

**Run this immediately after §5 passes and before the loop.** It needs no
analog board.

S39 measured chip 1 at **114.9 %** and chip 2 at **103.1 %** of the
327,680-cycle per-block budget — **with every input at full scale by accident**,
because S39-4's one-bit error turned each converter's noise floor into a
near-full-scale artefact and engaged every gate and compressor on chip 1 for
the whole measurement. On the shipping configuration (`DSP4_DYN_LUT=0`) driven
costs **+42.5 points on chip 1 and +29.3 on chip 2** over silent-loaded.

| | S39, capture broken | **expected now** |
|---|---|---|
| chip 1 | 114.9 % | **≈ 76 %** |
| chip 2 | 103.1 % | **≈ 90 %** |

**If it falls to roughly those numbers**, then on the shipping configuration a
quiet D24 fits chip 1 and does not fit chip 2, and the first time anybody
speaks into a microphone it stops fitting either. **If it does not fall, the
load is somewhere the record does not have it, and that is a new finding** —
write it up as one rather than re-arguing the sheet.

Write both numbers into `MW/D24/DSP/dsp4-capacity-decision-20260913.md`. The
decision it asks PW for is unchanged either way — a mixer is not specified at
idle — but the number PW decides against should be the honest one.

## 7. The loop — **PW plugs the cable into these connectors**

S42 found the output map was deranged in two independent ways and fixed both
in `dsp.csv`. **The connector that carries AUX 1 has changed**, so the patch
lead PW ran for S39 was — correctly — in the wrong socket, and the same cable
in the same socket now carries the right signal for a different reason.

| signal | plug into | why |
|---|---|---|
| **AUX OUT 1** | rear XLR marked **Aux Out A1** | `C2_AUX_OUT_01` moved `DAC_01` → `DAC_08`; the OUT_1-8 block is reversed (U81 ch *n* → rear `OUT_(9−n)`), so aux *n* now reaches the XLR marked Aux Out A*n*. **Before S42, AUX 1 came out of Aux Out A8.** |
| **MAIN OUT L** | rear XLR **J56** (Main Out L) | `C2_MAIN_OUT_01` moved `DAC_13` → `DAC_12`. **`DAC_13` is NOT CONNECTED** (U92 pins 32/33 are one-pin nets) — Main Out 1 was on a dead DAC channel. |
| **MAIN OUT R** | rear XLR **J57** (Main Out R) | `C2_MAIN_OUT_02` moved `DAC_14` (Centre/LF) → `DAC_11`. |

Full 27-row table with the copper traced: `docs/d24-dac-lane-xlr-candidate-20260913.md`
(filed as a **defs candidate**, not landed).

PW's loop for S40 is **AUX OUTPUT 1 → MIC INPUT 1**, no pad (the mic pre at
step 0 is designed to take aux max output). With §5 passed and the cable in
**Aux Out A1**, aux 1 open vs muted must move MIC 1's lane by the loop
transfer — **not by the <1 dB with no ordering that S39-6 measured**, which is
fully explained by the cable having been in a socket carrying silent aux 8.

**If it still does not move**, the split is clean and narrow, and it is one
probe, not a hunt: the DSP writes the right slot (§5.3 proved it) and the slot
reaches `U81 ch8 → analog J45 → rear XLR OUT_01 / phonejack J4 tip` (the table
proves the copper). **The probe is `analog J45` pin 2 against the AUX 1 XLR
pin 2, with the DAC driving.** Everything upstream of J45 is excluded by
construction. Write it down for PW with the expected level and stop that arm.

## 8. Then S40's gates 3 and 4, unchanged

Unchanged from the S40 dispatch block, and both depend on §7:

**Gate 3 — the loop heard.** Aux 1 open vs muted must move MIC 1's RX lane by
the loop transfer, not by <1 dB. There is no in-kernel oscillator (S39-9):
`C1_NOISE` has no oscillator and no consumer, and `scope.asm` does impulse and
step only. Use those; if a tone is cheap (a 32-sample sine table on the AUX 1
path), say what it costs against §6's numbers before adding it.

**Gate 4 — the first measurements in dBFS, only if gate 3 passes.** PW's
ruling of 2026-09-12 is binding and the full-scale values come **first**:

* **(a) DAC side** — what 0 dBFS in the AUX 1 TX lane is at the AUX 1 XLR, in
  V peak and dBu, derived from the AK4458 reference and the output stage on
  the analog OUT/LIN_OUT sheets.
* **(b) ADC side** — the MIC 1 XLR level that produces 0 dBFS on the RX lane
  at gain step 0, from the AK5558 reference, the pre's step-0 gain and the
  2V5 common-mode interface.
* **(c) the loop transfer at step 0** = (a)→(b) predicted, then measured as
  dBFS_in → dBFS_out.

**Never a raw sample count alone.** Then: noise floor of MIC 1 with AUX 1
muted; the gain-step law over steps 0, 8, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48, 56, 63 via
`app cli chain-set 27 ch1:mute=0,gain=<n>` (channel 1 only, phantom never,
log every chain-set) against the app's `PreampGainTable`; electrical
end-to-end latency step → return in samples; impulse → magnitude response if
the impulse path is clean. Each with the estimator's own uncertainty stated.
**Leave the loop channel at step 0, unmuted, when done.**

Keep the derivations in the write-up.
