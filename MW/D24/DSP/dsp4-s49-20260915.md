# S49 — the part measures itself: a sine oscillator, an RMS / THD+N / noise / crosstalk engine, and an FFT that agrees with them

**WHAT SELF-TEST CAN NOW DO ON ITS OWN.** The desk can generate a precision
sine at any frequency the contract carries, inject it into any channel
strip's input, and publish that strip's total RMS, its THD+N, its
noise-plus-distortion level and its crosstalk against a neighbour — in dB,
through the `Test[1-1]*` cells, **with no external instrument, no cable and
nothing analog in the loop.** On the part it measures the D24 channel strip
**flat to ±0.01 dB from 100 Hz to 20 kHz** at a THD+N floor between **−103
and −141 dB**, and it does it through the same digital signal path the
product uses — trim, HPF, EQ, gate, compressor, tube, delay, fader. On its
first run it found two things nobody was looking for: the shipping strip's
compressor knee, read straight off a transfer curve (**4:1 above −20 dBFS**,
exactly the `threshold_db=-20.0;ratio=4.0` the graph declares), and **a gate
whose closed state is 0.001 dB of attenuation instead of 60 dB** — a
one-word unit error that makes the gate inert on every strip of every image
nobody has written gate cells to. A host-side FFT on the same samples, by a
completely different method, agrees with the node to **0.00 dB on level and
0.00 dB on THD+N**.

**WHAT STILL NEEDS THE ANALOG LOOP.** Everything past the DSP's own pins.
This measures a digital signal path end to end; it cannot see the DAC, the
output stage, the XLR, the cable, the preamp or the ADC, and it says nothing
about any of them. S48 established that the break on this unit is at or
after the DAC and that the DSP reaches the DAC lane on demand; nothing here
changes that or touches it. The list of what waits on the analog loop is §11.

## 2. The method, and the two decisions that make the numbers worth having

**The oscillator is a coupled-form ("magic circle") resonator**, four float
operations a sample:

```
x <- x + k*y ;   y <- y - k*x        k = 2*sin(pi*f/fs)
```

Its two states are a sine/cosine pair at the commanded frequency — not in
exact quadrature, w/2 apart, which is why the measurement solves a 2×2 rather
than assuming orthogonality. No table: a quarter-wave sine table good enough
to measure THD against costs thousands of words of a chip 1 with hundreds
left, and the interpolation error it leaves IS a harmonic — it would land in
the very number the node exists to publish. This costs eleven words of state
and its error is white.

**The measurement removes the fundamental by LEAST SQUARES, not by a notch.**
A notch deep enough to see −100 dB needs coefficient precision a Q4.28 biquad
does not have and a settling time the window would have to wait out. The
oscillator's own two state blocks are the reference instead:

```
[ Sss Ssc ] [a]   [Sxs]
[ Ssc Scc ] [b] = [Sxc]        e[n] = x[n] - a*s[n] - b*c[n]
```

Three properties follow, and they are what make the figure trustworthy:

* **The oscillator's frequency accuracy does not enter.** The reference is the
  same waveform that was injected, so a `k` a part in 10⁷ off the commanded
  frequency cancels exactly. Nothing assumes the frequency fits a whole
  number of cycles into the window either — no window function, no leakage
  term, no coherent-sampling requirement.
* **Any fixed delay through the path cancels.** A delayed sinusoid is a linear
  combination of `s` and `c` at the same frequency, which is exactly what the
  2×2 fits. The tap can be zero, one or fifty samples behind the injection.
* **The oscillator's own distortion is INSIDE the answer, not cancelled out of
  it.** The residual is everything that is not a sinusoid at f, whoever made
  it. That is the conservative direction, it is what a self-test should
  publish, and it is why the host-side FFT and the node can be expected to
  agree rather than to differ by the generator.

**THE RESIDUAL IS ACCUMULATED PER SAMPLE AND NEVER BY SUBTRACTING TWO LARGE
NUMBERS.** `Sxx − a·Sxs − b·Sxc` is algebraically the residual energy and is
useless in float32: both terms are of order `Sxx`, so the difference carries
the format's relative precision, 1.2e-7, i.e. **−69 dB** — and every THD+N
reading below that would be the subtraction's rounding and nothing else.
Accumulating `Σe²` sample by sample cancels x against a·s+b·c at the SAMPLE's
own magnitude, where an ulp is 1.2e-7 of one sample and not of four thousand
of them. The price is one window of latency: e[n] needs (a,b) and (a,b) needs
a finished window, so each window subtracts the PREVIOUS window's fit. They
start at zero, which is why **the first window after any change reads
ThdResult 0.00 dB and the second one is the answer.** `_meas_seq_` is how a
caller tells them apart.

**What each read-back means** (dBFS is against 1.0 in Q4.28, converter full
scale — S48 §7.2 — and NOT against the 8.0 accumulator ceiling; the two are
18.06 dB apart):

| cell | value |
|---|---|
| `RmsResult` | `10·log10(Sxx/N)` — total RMS of MeasChan, dBFS |
| `NoiseResult` | `10·log10(See/N)` — noise+distortion level, dBFS |
| `ThdResult` | `10·log10(See/Sxx)` — THD+N relative to total, dB |
| `XtalkResult` | `10·log10(Sdd/Ssrc)` — XtalkDst against XtalkSrc, dB |

With the oscillator OFF the reference blocks are zeroed, the 2×2 goes
singular, a = b = 0 and e[n] IS x[n] — so `NoiseResult == RmsResult ==` the
channel's own floor and `ThdResult` reads 0.00 dB. That is a definition, not
an accident: it makes NoiseResult one number with one meaning whether or not
a tone is present, and it makes a noise measurement and a THD+N measurement
the same measurement.

## 3. Two arithmetic errors caught before the part saw them, and one it caught

**THE INVARIANT'S SIGN.** The magic circle conserves

```
E = x^2 + k*x*y + y^2          (PLUS)
```

`M^T Q M = Q` gives β = **+k**. The first implementation used the
symmetrical-looking `x² + y² − k·x·y`, which is not conserved by the
recurrence at all. Below a few kHz that is nearly right and the oscillator
behaves; at 10 kHz the amplitude reached 1.26 within a second, at 20 kHz it
diverged to a float overflow. **`tools/dsp/test_node_ref.py` caught it at the
desk**, before an image was built, in its first run: peak 1.0005 / 1.0013 /
1.056 / 1.26 across 20 Hz to 10 kHz and then a crash. Caught again on the
part afterwards, because a bench arm ran against a tree generated before the
fix landed — 15 kHz read +2.1 dB and 20 kHz read −168 dBFS with a NaN beside
it. **Both readings are withdrawn; every number in this document is from the
corrected pair.** The reference is committed and runs in seconds:
`python3 tools/dsp/test_node_ref.py`.

**THE AMPLITUDE SCALE.** On the ellipse E = 1 the state reaches
1/cos(πf/fs) — 1.002 at 1 kHz and **3.86 at 20 kHz** — so an unscaled output
is 11.7 dB hot at the top of the range. The invariant stays at 1 (which keeps
the Newton correction to two operations) and the published block is scaled by
cos(πf/fs), derived from the same z = x² the sine polynomial already has.
Result on the part: **RmsResult −23.01 dBFS at every frequency from 100 Hz to
20 kHz for a −20 dBFS peak command**, where −23.0103 is the exact answer.

**THE TAP'S DISPATCH, caught on the part.** The first `_test_meas_tap` was a
first-match dispatch: MeasChan, then XtalkSrc, then XtalkDst, first one wins.
A strip named as BOTH MeasChan and XtalkSrc therefore fed only the
measurement, `Σx² on XtalkSrc` stayed at zero, and XtalkResult published
`10·log10(Sdd/floor)` — **+220.03 dB, measured on the part**, or −317 dB with
the roles reversed. Neither is a crosstalk figure and both look like one. The
cells are independent selectors and the routine now treats them that way;
naming one strip in two of them is the NORMAL arrangement (measure the source
while watching a neighbour).

**Arm 0's chip 2 is `7b1311e8…` — the S42 chip-2 image, BYTE FOR BYTE.** The
whole of S49 touches chip 2 by not one byte when the flag is off, which is

the cells and not because the nodes are switched on. A dispatch entry naming
a symbol that exists only under a build flag does not link, so the sixteen
control/result words are declared unconditionally and everything else — the
blocks, the table, every instruction — is inside the guard.

**Identification at four independent levels**, S48 §6's discipline:

1. md5 of the staged pair, verified on both sides of the copy;
2. the symbol map: 6,155 symbols shipping → **6,172** on arm 0 (the sixteen
   contract words) → **6,197** on arm A (`_osc_blk_q_C1_TEST_OSC`,
   `_test_osc_inject`, `_test_meas_tap` and the rest of the guarded content);
3. the build banner, `*** SELF-TEST BUILD: DSP4_TEST_NODES=1 ***`;
4. **the part itself** — `DIAG_BUILD_CFG2` reads `0xC2010244` on arm 0 and
   `0xC3010244` on arms A and B, and `dsp4_buildcfg.py` prints
   `DSP4_TEST_NODES = 1, shipping is 0`.

Level 4 needed a bit and `DIAG_BUILD_CFG2` had none: bits 23..0 are fully
allocated (S18 spent the last two) and 31..24 was the signature. Rather than
invent a third word for one bit, **the signature narrows from eight bits to
seven** — 31..25 stay `0b1100001` and bit 24 is the flag. The failure mode is
the one to want: a decoder still testing the whole top byte REJECTS a
self-test image's word outright rather than reading it and quietly reporting
the wrong decimate. That is not a prediction — the first S49 image did exactly
that against the eight-bit test and said so on both chips.

**±0.01 dB from 100 Hz to 20 kHz.** The two low points are not the

scores against the float64 mean square of the samples actually injected
rather than against A²/2, so the test measures the arithmetic and not the
window. 85 ms is the window that gives the THD+N floor below: a longer one
costs float32 accumulator precision faster than it buys cycles — measured,
512 blocks gives −111 dB and 1024 gives −102 dB against 256 blocks' −115 dB.

Below −20 dBFS the strip is **exactly unity to two decimal places** at a
THD+N floor of −110 to −122 dB. Above it, every 3 dB of input gives
0.74–0.76 dB of output — **4:1**, and `C1_COMP_05` declares
`threshold_db=-20.0;ratio=4.0;knee_db=6.0`. The knee is where the graph says
it is and the ratio is what the graph says it is, measured rather than
assumed, by the desk, on itself.

**The THD+N floor is the INSTRUMENT'S, not the signal's, in the linear
region** — see §8, where the host-side FFT reads −150.63 dB on the same
capture the node scored at −115.70 dB. The float32 reference predicts
−115.25 dB for this window length, and the part reads −114.6 to −116.1 at
1 kHz across five runs. The node is reporting its own arithmetic floor and
the FFT proves it.

**Noise, oscillator off.** Strip 5's post-fader block with the input
demonstrably static (§10): **RmsResult = NoiseResult = −115.93 dBFS,
ThdResult 0.00 dB** — the degenerate branch behaving exactly as designed,
and a clean measurement of the strip's own arithmetic noise floor with no
converter in it. A muted strip reads **−336.12 dBFS**, which is the energy
floor: exactly zero, as a muted digital strip should be.

**Crosstalk, and the arm that makes it mean something.** With
`dsp4_apply_strip.py`'s own preamble every strip but the one it sets up is
MUTED, so strip 6's block is exactly zero and XtalkResult reads the
arithmetic floor — true, and not a measurement of anything. Bringing strip 6
up to unity first (`tools/pi/dsp4_s49_cap.py`'s companion probe; the writes
are read back) makes it a live neighbour:

| | ch 6 RMS | XtalkResult |
|---|---:|---:|
| tone on 5 at −6 dBFS, seq 1633 | −116.16 dBFS | **−97.50 dB** |
| seq 1634 | −116.26 dBFS | **−97.62 dB** |
| seq 1635 | −116.08 dBFS | **−97.44 dB** |
| **negative arm — oscillator OFF** | −116.14 dBFS | **+0.01 dB** |
| | −115.82 dBFS | +0.36 dB |
| | −116.31 dBFS | −0.45 dB |

The negative arm is the one that makes the −97.5 dB readable: with no tone
both strips sit on their own floors and the ratio is 1, i.e. 0 dB. With the
tone the ratio is −97.5 dB and it repeats to a tenth. **It is an UPPER BOUND
and not a crosstalk figure**: whatever leaks from strip 5 into strip 6 is
below strip 6's own content, so the honest statement is *no crosstalk is
detectable above −97.5 dB* — and with strip 6 muted the same measurement
reads exactly zero, which is what a digital path with no shared state should
read. A control that must read 0.00 dB — XtalkSrc = XtalkDst = 5 — reads
**0.00 dB**.

**The sweep cells work, and the host cannot follow them.** `SweepOn=1,
SweepStep=16` walked the oscillator up the cell's own 128-position Log law
and stopped itself at code 112 (8,845.03 Hz), the last step below 127. The
stepping is DSP-paced — TEST_OSC advances on the window serial TEST_MEAS
publishes, so a step and the window that scored the previous frequency can
never be off by one. **The host missed the first six steps**, and that is an
instrument limit worth writing down: the parameter link serves one register
per audio block and `dsp4_scope.rd()` votes over up to twelve asks, so
reading the serial plus four results plus the frequency takes about 350 ms
against an 85 ms window. **A sweep should be driven one frequency at a time
over this link** — write `OscFreq`, wait two windows, read — which is what
the frequency table in §5 was taken with. The DSP-paced sweep is for a host
that polls a subset, or for a longer window.

**USING the nodes costs +1,520 cycles/block average and +903 worst-block —

because it is the same image, the same boot, and one cell write between the

each plus two idle node calls ≈ **640 cycles/block, 0.2 % of budget.**

**The 33 missed blocks on chip 2 are not S49's**, and the same fact is what
says so: chip 2 carries none of this code and sits at 99.5–100.4 % of budget

consequence of the self-test nodes.

`shipping.config` names the flag at 0 with these numbers beside it. **Turning
it on is PW's ruling, not this file's** — and it also needs the addresses
landed (§9), because without them no host can reach the cells.

## 8. Gate 4 — the FFT, and the agreement

`tools/pi/dsp4_fft.py`, **stdlib only on purpose**: the bench CM4 has no
numpy and no matplotlib, and a tool that only runs somewhere else is not a
CM4-side tool. The transform is a radix-2 FFT in `cmath` (numpy is used when
importable, purely for speed, and the tool says which arithmetic produced the
answer); the PNG is written by a small encoder at the bottom of the file.
`--selftest` scores it against closed-form synthetic signals and passes.

**The window is a 7-term Blackman-Harris and that is a measurement decision,
not a taste.** The capture is not coherent — nothing makes the oscillator's
frequency a multiple of fs/N and TEST_MEAS deliberately does not require it —
so whatever the window leaks is counted as distortion. The 4-term window this
tool carried first leaks to −92 dB, which sounds like plenty and is not:
integrated across a half spectrum it put the instrument's own THD+N floor at
**−97 dB** with a 4-bin band and −111 dB with a 16-bin one — ABOVE the node's
−115 dB floor, so the FFT would have been the limiting instrument in the very
comparison it exists to make. The 7-term window leaks below −180 dB and the
measured floor on a synthetic pure tone is −140 dB to below what float64 can
represent.

**The normalisation is Parseval's, so the two instruments quote the same
dBFS.** Dividing every bin by `n·Σw²` makes the one-sided sum of the spectrum
equal the mean square, which is exactly what `RmsResult` publishes. A
full-scale sine reads −3.01 dBFS on both. The obvious alternative — coherent
gain — is right for reading one bin's amplitude and wrong for summing a band,
and it was 0.01 dB out on the 4-term window and 1.18 dB out on the 7-term
one, which is how it was caught.

**Arm B, `C1_FDR_05`, 1024 samples, 1 kHz at −6 dBFS peak — the compressed
regime, where there is something real to measure:**

| | `Test[1-1]*` cell | `dsp4_fft.py` | difference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| level | RmsResult **−18.64 dBFS** | fundamental **−18.64 dBFS** | **0.00 dB** |
| THD+N | ThdResult **−55.75 dB** | **−55.75 dB** | **0.00 dB** |
| noise+distortion | NoiseResult **−74.39 dBFS** | **−74.40 dBFS** | **0.01 dB** |

Two methods with nothing in common — a least-squares fit in the time domain
on the DSP in float32, a windowed DFT on the host in float64 — agreeing to a
hundredth of a dB. **The stated tolerance is ±0.5 dB and the measurement beat
it by a factor of fifty.**

And the FFT says what the node cannot: the residual is an **odd-harmonic
ladder**, h3 at −55.93 dBc, h5 at −70.39, h7 at −78.28, h9 at −111.32 — the
signature of a symmetric compression characteristic, not of clipping. It also
shows spurs at 10,968–11,062 Hz and 12,937–13,031 Hz at about −98 dBc, which
are 12 kHz ± 1 kHz, i.e. intermodulation with something at fs/4. Recorded,
not chased.

**At −20 dBFS, in the linear region, the two DISAGREE and the disagreement is
the useful part:** ThdResult −115.70 dB against the FFT's −150.63 dB. The
node is reporting **its own arithmetic floor** — the float32 reference
predicts −115.25 dB for this window — and the FFT, which is not limited by
it, proves the signal is 35 dB cleaner than the node can resolve. That is the
right way round for a self-test: the published figure is conservative.

**The negative arm** — oscillator off, same capture — produced a THD+N of
**+11.33 dB** and an SNR of −11.33 dB, because with no tone the largest band
is simply the largest noise bin. It is not wrong, and it reads exactly like a
measurement. `dsp4_fft.py` now tests whether the fundamental clears the
median bin by 20 dB and says **NO TONE IN THIS CAPTURE** when it does not,
naming `total` as the only figure that stays valid. Its `total` on that
capture, −116.96 dBFS, sits 0.64 dB from the node's −116.32 dBFS over a
different window at a different time, which is the agreement a noise
measurement gets.

Figures and captures are committed beside this document in
`MW/D24/DSP/s49/`: `s49_fdr05_-6.png` (the harmonic ladder),
`s49_fdr05_-20.png` (the linear region), `s49_fdr05_off.png`, and the three
capture files they were made from.

## 9. Gate 3 — the contract proposal, and the dispatch's one wrong premise

`proposals/CONTRACT-PROPOSAL-S49.md`, with the pair per product under
`proposals/defs/products/<p>/`.

**The dispatch states the thirteen cells are "all in `dsp-unmapped.csv` as
no-graph-node today". They are not — not on any of the four products, and not
in any `_matrix.csv` either.** The thirteen rows exist only in the cell
library. `defs/tools/def_master.py` gates the `Test` prefix on the def keys
`util` (scope) and `util.osc` (function), and no product def declares either,
so the per-product masters carry no `Test` row and `gen_dsp.py` — whose
proposal is the intersection of the graph's expansion with the product's cell
SET — drops them before either file is written. A cell no product defines is
in neither; it is not "unmapped", it is **not present**.

So the proposal is two things: **a def change only the hub can land** (two
lines per product, `util,1` and `util.osc,1`, filed as
`proposals/defs/products/<p>/<p>.csv`) and **the addresses that follow from
it**. Measured rather than asserted: those two keys add exactly **13 master
rows and 13 expanded cells on each of D32, D24, D16 and D12** and open no
other family.

The addresses are strictly additive — **0 cells moved, 0 removed, 0 changed
rows across all 5,766 / 3,975 / 2,572 / 1,778 shared rows, `dsp-unmapped.csv`
unchanged to the row, 318 multi-cell addresses before and after, chip 2
untouched.** They are free because of where the nodes were added: the chip-1
allocator packs sequentially, so a node inserted anywhere but the end moves
the whole map, and both self-test nodes are the last chip-1 rows in
`dsp.csv`. Same rule S22-4 followed for the matrix sends.

**`gen_dsp.py --check-proposal` still passes on this tree and that is not a
mistake** — it is the same fact showing through: the thirteen cells are in no
product's cell set, so the check cannot see them and reports OK either way.
Relying on it here would be reading a green light aimed at a different
question. `./proposals/regen-s49-proposal.sh --check` asks the open one — it
stages the def change in a scratch directory, re-runs defs' OWN

## 10. What the self-test found that nobody was looking for

### 10.1 The gate's range is in the wrong unit, and the gate is inert

`C1_GATE_nn`'s kernel reads `_gate_range_` **as dB** — it clamps to 0..60 and
computes `10^(-dB/20)` — and the generated `.var` initialises it to **0.001**,
with a comment calling it a "linear floor". The conversion's own comment says
"the convention was dB before it was ever measured". So an un-written gate
computes `10^(-0.001/20) = 0.999885` as its CLOSED gain: **0.001 dB of
attenuation where the graph row says `range_db=60.0`.**

Read straight off the part, strip 5, 1 kHz at −60 dBFS peak (−63 dBFS RMS)
against a −40 dBFS threshold:

| symbol | value |
|---|---|
| `_gate_on_C1_GATE_05` | 1 |
| `_gate_threshold_C1_GATE_05` | −40 (float, dB) — correct |
| `_gate_range_C1_GATE_05` | **0.001** |
| `_gate_rngq_C1_GATE_05` | `0x0FFF873C` = **0.999885** in Q4.28 |
| `_gate_envelope_C1_GATE_05` | 0.000874 — below threshold |
| `_gate_gain_C1_GATE_05` | **0.999885** |
| `_gate_gain_target_q_C1_GATE_05` | **0.999885** |

`10^(-0.001/20) × 2²⁸ = 0x0FFF8749`; the part holds `0x0FFF873C`, thirteen
counts away, which is `_exp2q_fx`'s own approximation error. **The gate is
detecting correctly and closing correctly onto a closed gain of unity.** It
is why the level sweep in §6 shows exactly 0.00 dB at −60 dBFS where a gate
with `range_db=60` would show 60 dB of attenuation.

**NOT FIXED IN THIS SESSION, deliberately.** The one-line change is to
initialise `_gate_range_` from the row's own `range_db` (60.0) instead of a
hardcoded 0.001 — but it changes audio on every strip of every image, it
would invalidate the byte-for-byte control arm this session rests on, and a
gate that suddenly closes 60 dB on a quiet channel wants a bench arm of its
own. Filed as findings **S49-8**, HIGH, with the measurement above. On a D24,
where nothing on the host writes per-strip node state (S38-5), every strip is
running on that initialiser.

`_gate_hold_count_C1_GATE_05` also reads negative while the gate is closed
(`0xFFFEED10`). Noted, not investigated.

### 10.2 The converters are not converting, and the bench says so

**All 24 chip-1 RX lanes read STATIC** — `dsp4_inscan.py`, 8 reads each at
~10 ms spacing, `MOVING 0 / STATIC 24 / UNREADABLE 0`, lane 5 stuck at
`0x00000040`. That is the state with AN_EN low (§11) and it is **why the
noise figures in §6 are the strip's own arithmetic and not a converter floor.**

It matters to exactly two numbers and to no others. Every other figure in
this document comes from a digitally injected signal that **REPLACES** the
strip's input block after the input kernel has filled it — whatever the
converter sent is discarded — so AN_EN, the converters and the analog rails
are irrelevant to the level sweep, the frequency sweep, the THD+N floor, the
compressor curve and the FFT agreement. The two it does qualify are the strip
floor (−115.93 dBFS, which is now a clean measurement of the DSP's own noise
with a provably static input) and the crosstalk bound (−97.5 dB, which stands
as a bound either way).
