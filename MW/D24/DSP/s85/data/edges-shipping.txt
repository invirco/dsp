A lane whose count depends on WHEN in the bit period it is sampled is a lane whose data is not stable across the window the DSP samples in -- the converter's launch phase, plus the round trip out and back, has moved the data edge onto one of these two sampling points. That is a LOGIC fix: capture the lane on the edge it is valid on and re-launch it to the DSP.
