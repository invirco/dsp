# S38 — the converter-clock flash, and what it cost chip 1

**2026-09-12, MW-D24-2 (192.168.1.219), D24 Analog rev B attached and live.**

The flash landed on the first attempt and the unit is on
`s37_shipping_step0.c62c024714f2`. Everything the flash was supposed to be
invisible to, it was not: **chip 1 drops one audio block in nine on the new
bitstream and none on the old.** That is established with a rollback control
rather than a single before/after, and four candidate causes are ruled out by
measurement. The mechanism is not found, and the instrument that would find it
does not exist on this image.

Audio still does not pass. That has nothing to do with the flash, and the chain
walk says exactly where it stops.

## 1. Gate 1 — the interlock, read rather than assumed

`./logic_flash.sh --dry-run s37_shipping_step0.c62c024714f2.svf` → **exit 0**,
every gate passed, nothing written. The interlock was read from the pin **with
`matrix-app` running**:

    AN_EN (26) : 26: op -- pd | lo // GPIO26 = output
    AN_EN state: low

This is what closes S35-1 on the bench. The gated app
(`a509053ca0a999de1ee7aabd9a234089`, confirmed by md5 on the unit) does not
assert GPIO26, and says so in its own log:

    Boot.Init() - AN_EN gated (PW 2026-09-10): analogAutoEnable=false,
    chain latch=CM4 GPIO 27 — bring-up runs after S_RUN

So `--stop-app` was never needed for the interlock and `--an-en-waived` was
never used. The rollback was verified present and hashed before the first byte:
`dsp4_logic.a1f6672af6c3.svf` = `dd1e09185804cb2e451d5089cdd56be3`.

One wording correction for the record: the app ALSO logs `AnalogBringUp: AN_EN
STAYS LOW — digital clocks not proven stable`, listing three gates it cannot
see. It states plainly that it does not boot the DSPs — *"SPI2 slave boot runs
outside this process (dsp4_boot.py, dsp repo) — no boot-state query"* — so those
gates read `Unknown` whatever the bench does. That is expected, not a fault.

## 3. Gate 4 — the bar, and the control that was worth the two extra flashes

**A pre-flash baseline was taken before anything was written.** That turned out
to be the most useful ten minutes of the session: chip 1 read 90,019 blocks in
30.0 s with a **zero** overrun delta, matching S27's reference exactly. So the
boot path was known-good *before* the CPLD changed.

After the flash, on the same images (md5-verified identical in every arm) and
the same recipe, chip 1 read a **9957** overrun delta — and then 9957 again, and
9957 again, on three consecutive windows. Two fresh boots gave 6639 and 6673 per
20 s. That is 331.9–333.7 blocks/s against a 3000/s block rate: **11.06 %, one
block in nine**, deterministic to three figures.

A single-sample baseline against a five-sample result is not an attribution, so
the designed control was run: roll back to `a1f6672af6c3`, measure, re-flash
step 0, measure again.

| bitstream | boots | chip 1 delta | chip 2 delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| `a1f6672af6c3` (old) | 6 | 0 × 6 — **clean** | 0, 1492, 0, 1493, 1493 — **3 of 5 fail** |
| `s37_shipping_step0` | 5 | ~332 /s × 5 — **all fail** | 0 — clean |

Two results, not one.

**Chip 1 is broken by the new bitstream.** Six clean boots on the old, five
failing boots on the new, at a rate reproducible to three figures.

**Chip 2 was never clean on the old one.** It fails 3 boots in 5 on
`a1f6672af6c3` at ~74.6 blocks/s, and is clean on step 0. So the bitstream this
unit has run for three weeks does not meet the bar either, and any earlier "both
chips, zero overruns" reading was a 2-in-5 draw. The honest summary is that both
chips sit close to the per-block budget and small changes tip them either way.

### What it is not

Ruled out by measurement, not by argument:

* **Not the core clock.** 999.96 Hz on both chips, ratio to nominal 0.99996,
  identical to five figures. Same cycles available.
* **Not the block rate.** 90,016 ± 3 blocks per 30 s window on both chips in
  both arms.
* **Not the transport.** `SPORT0_ERR_A 0x00000000`, `DMA0_STAT 0x00006200`,
  both chips, both arms.
* **Not input data.** All twelve `_rx_slot_C1_IN_nn` lanes read a static zero on
  BOTH bitstreams. The attractive story — clocked converters mean real audio
  means the dynamics leave their cheap branch — is refuted at the DSP input.

What the flash changed is small and is not a DSP input: pins 142/141 from inputs
(dead straps feeding only the `_unused` reduction) to outputs driving
`bck8`/`fs8`, and `snake_out`/`dac_main` from constantly driven to high-Z with
weak pull-ups on a D24. That is why the result is surprising.

### Why it was not settled

"More work per block" and "the same work against a block boundary that moved"
are separated by the per-block cycle count. `_proc_cyc`, `_proc_cyc_max` and
`_proc_passes` read **0 on both chips** — including healthy chip 2, which is how
we know that is an uninstrumented build rather than a hung loop. **S39's first
job is a `DSP4_TCOUNT` build of the same pair**: a desk build and one boot, and
it turns this from an attribution into a mechanism.

## 4. Gate 5 — channel 1, and why a mic on it will be silent

**The shipping configuration routes channel 1 nowhere.** `dsp4_config.py
--product d24` writes only the product SCOPE — `CFG_PRODUCT_ID`,
`CFG_CHAN_MASK`, `CFG_AUX_MASK`, `CFG_OUT_MUX`, the patch list, `CFG_COMMIT`
(51 words to chip 1, 5 to chip 2). There is no per-strip routing in it.

So the route was set explicitly, by cell name out of `landed-d24.json`
(`tools/pi/dsp4_s38_ch1_main.py`). Every strip is taken off the main bus and
muted two independent ways first — one inert cell would otherwise leave the bus
live and read as a pedestal under the mic — and then:

| cell | node | value |
|---|---|---|
| `Chan001Level001` | `C1_FDR_01` | `1.0f` (unity) |
| `Chan001Pan001` | `C1_FDR_01` | centre → equal L and R |
| `Chan001Mute001` | `C1_FDR_01` | 0 |
| `Chan001MainOn001` | `C1_RTG_01` | 1 |
| `Main001Level001` | `C2_MAIN_FDR` | `1.0f` |
| `Main001Mute001` | `C2_MAIN_FDR` | 0 |

Strips 25–32 have no cells in the D24 contract at all (S5-7), so
`dsp4_silence_2532.py` closes them through the D32 rows.

**It is still silent, and the chain walk says where it stops.** Injecting a 0.25
step at `_rx_slot_C1_IN_01`:

    _buf_C1_IN_01     +0.250000000   the injection is there
    _buf_C1_GAIN_01   +0.000000000   it dies HERE
    _buf_C1_FDR_01    +0.000000000

`C1_GAIN_01`'s entire node state block is zero — `_gain_coeff`, `_gain_target`,
`_gain_step`, `_gain_frames`, `_gain_q`, `_mute`, `_polarity`,
`_tap_post_trim`. That is a state block nothing has ever written, not a gain set
to zero: `_mute` would be 1 if it had been muted. Writes to `Chan001Gain001` at
ramp id 1, 0 and 2, polled ten seconds each, move neither coefficient nor
target, and `gainfix.py` reports `strip 1 still 0x00000000`.

**A method note, because it nearly produced a wrong finding.** The first
read-back suggested parameter writes were landing on neither chip — a serious
claim. It was wrong. `dsp4_conform.py --phase presence` over addresses 0–90 on
chip 1 returns `ECHO: 81, part healthy at exit: True`: the parameter store takes
every write and reads it back. The mistake was verifying a parameter write
against the node's working symbol instead of against the store. The store is
written; the node never applies it. Only the second defect is real.

So the open question for the first audio pass is **what writes per-strip node
state on a D24.** It is not `dsp4_config.py`, and it is not `matrix-app` as
observed here: `MW/D24/MX/_matrix.csv` carries no DSP address backfill at all
(0 of 4985 rows have `DspAdd`), so the app has no DSP address for
`Chan001Gain001`. On the bench the cell is reachable only through
`landed-d24.json`. Whether that is deliberate for D24 or a gap is a
defs/contract question, not a bench one.

**And the converters are not delivering.** All twelve input lanes are static zero
on both bitstreams. A lane fed by a clocked converter moves on its own noise
floor with nothing plugged in; these do not move at all. A clock alone is not
sufficient — and the CM4 cannot see whether the clock is even there.

> **PROBE PLEASE: J18 P37 = 12.288 MHz, P38 = 48 kHz — or U3 pins 142/141 at
> the source.** This is the measurement that closes S34-1. It cannot be taken
> from the CM4 (S35-5: no CM4 GPIO reaches C1/L0/TEST1–4).

## 5. Cold start — re-establishing this state after a power cycle

From a cold power-up, on the desk and on the bench:

```bash
# 1. desk: confirm what is on the part (no write; exits 0 if the gates pass)
cd tools/pi && ./logic_flash.sh --dry-run s37_shipping_step0.c62c024714f2.svf
#    expect: AN_EN state: low, IDCODE before: 0x020a30dd, exit 0
#    the part's identity is the flash log; the artifact has no design ID

# 2. bench: stop the app and hand the pins back (S8-3 — NOT `a0` on 6 and 24)
ssh app@192.168.1.219
sudo systemctl stop matrix-app; sleep 2
sudo pinctrl set 6,24 op dh        # the two chip selects, DEASSERTED
sudo pinctrl set 8,12 ip           # the two SPI_RDY lines
sudo pinctrl set 7,9,10,11,22,23,25 a0

# 3. boot and configure (re-assert 6,24 before every tool — a python tool
#    that exits releases the line)
cd /home/app/s38pre
python3 dsp4_boot.py --dir /home/app/s38pre
python3 dsp4_checkchip.py                       # a wrong CHIP_ID makes all else fiction
sudo pinctrl set 6,24 op dh; python3 dsp4_config.py --product d24 --chip 1
sudo pinctrl set 6,24 op dh; python3 dsp4_config.py --product d24 --chip 2

# 4. route channel 1 to MAIN L/R at unity, and close strips 25-32
cd /home/app/dspboot
sudo pinctrl set 6,24 op dh; python3 /home/app/s38pre/dsp4_s38_ch1_main.py
sudo pinctrl set 6,24 op dh; python3 dsp4_silence_2532.py

# 5. prove it is streaming (30 s, both chips)
cd /home/app/s38pre
sudo pinctrl set 6,24 op dh; python3 dsp4_blk30.py 1 30
sudo pinctrl set 6,24 op dh; python3 dsp4_blk30.py 2 30
#    expect chip 2 BLK_OK; chip 1 BLK_FAIL at ~9600/30 s until S38-3 is fixed

# 6. give the unit back to the app
sudo systemctl start matrix-app
#    confirm: pinctrl get 26 -> lo
#    confirm in /home/app/logs/log:
#      "Boot.Init() - AN_EN gated ... chain latch=CM4 GPIO 27"
#      "Boot.Loop() - MCU boot verified: H1S1 / H1S3 / H1S4"
#    the MCU verify is a ~1-in-4 race (mx26 B13): restart until 3 of 3.
```

`/home/app/s38pre` holds the `blk_*` pair as `chip1.ldr` / `chip2.ldr`
(`ac65ad38…` / `e5dce9e4…`) plus this session's instruments. `~/dspboot` was not
written to.

## 6. Gate 6 — what was not started

**Neither.** Stated explicitly, as the dispatch asked:

* **The S37-3 re-take of the 82-sample bar / driven capacity rows — NOT
  started.** It is not possible on this base: step 0 ties `pcm_din` to `1'b0`,
  so there is no capture path and the arm cannot run at all. The manifest says
  so and this session confirms it rather than discovering it. It belongs to
  step 1.
* **The S37-2 codegen bench — NOT started.** The session's time went to the
  flash, the rollback control, and characterising S38-3. It is unaffected by
  anything here and remains a small, clean, testable job.

## 7. New instruments

Added to `tools/pi/`, all used for the readings above:

* `dsp4_blk30.py <chip> <s>` — the `blk_*` bar on one chip, from counter
  DELTAS with MAGIC bracketing both snapshots, and refusing to score at all
  unless `dsp4_block.py` is staged beside it.
* `dsp4_cclk.py <chip> <s>` — the diag-timer rate, MAGIC-guarded. Exists
  because `dsp4_diag.py --rate` is not (S38-7).
* `dsp4_inscan.py <chip>` — do the TDM input lanes MOVE. A stuck non-zero word
  is as dead as a stuck zero, so the test is variation, not magnitude.
* `dsp4_diagdump.py <chip>` — the full diag block through `DiagLink.resync()`,
  which phases on loaded links where the `dsp4_diag` CLI path refuses.
* `dsp4_s38_ch1_main.py` — channel 1 to MAIN L/R at unity, everything else
  silenced two ways first.
