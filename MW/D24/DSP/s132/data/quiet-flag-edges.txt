WHY IT MATTERS AND WHY IT IS NOT THE 09-27 FAULT. `d24_runall.quiet_hold` is a
`while os.path.exists(path)` loop polled at QUIET_POLL_S = 0.1 s, so a 41 ms gap
is usually missed -- but `PL.loop` also asks the hold ONCE before it lights each
indicator, and a call that lands in a gap lights the next button with the tone
about to play into the microphone AL1 reads. The hold was leaky by construction
and neither side could see it. It was never the cause of the NO DATA (that was
two stale tools; every leg died in 0.5 s and no acoustic window existed to
disturb), and it is fixed here because it is a real latent defect found on the
way.
