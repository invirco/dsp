# S9-2 Option A, built and proven on the part — and it is cheaper than it was costed

**The costing said Option A would cost ~640 cycles/block, 320 words of DM
and +16 samples of latency. On the part it costs ZERO cycles, ZERO DM, and
+16 samples PER CHIP. Both corrections matter and both are measured.**

Switch: `DSP4_TX_EARLY`, default **0**. Behind it because this is PW's
ruling, not the build's.

## 1. Why there is no staging block

The costing proposed gathering into scratch and copying that into the DMA
half at the top of the next period. **That copy is unnecessary and it would
not have worked**: a copy at the top of period N is still racing frame 0 of
period N, with 640 cycles of head start instead of 300,000.

There are TWO halves and the DDE touches each one every OTHER period. The
half the DDE is not clocking is idle for a whole period, so the core simply
writes that one:

* period N — the DDE clocks half A; the core fills half B
* period N+1 — the DDE clocks B, complete before the period began; the core
  fills A

No third buffer, no copy, no change to the DMA topology, and no change to
the one-interrupt-one-half mapping that the phase fix, the diagnostics and
the staircase scorer all rest on. It is four instructions in
`_blk_latch_bufs`, derived from the same ping/pong toggle the ISR uses, so
it cannot drift from it. `DSP4_TX_EARLY` requires `DSP4_BLK_LATCH` and the
assembler says so.

## 2. It is a PER-CHIP MASK, because the cost is per chip

`1` = chip 1's inter-chip TX, `2` = chip 2's converter TX, `3` = both. The
costing's "+16 samples" is right **per chip**; the signal crosses two
outbound regions on its way to the converters, so both chips is +32.

## 3. The transmit stamp, full D24 graph, Pi staircase playing, 192,000 frames

`DSP4_TXPROBE=1`, `_maincap` bitstream, `s9wire_run.sh`, `dsp4_tx_order.py`.

| arm | ordered | stamp delta histogram | blocks missed |
|---|--:|---|--:|
| `DSP4_TX_EARLY=0` (control) | **87.4999 %** | `[(1, 167999), (-31, 12000), (33, 12000)]` | 0 |
| `DSP4_TX_EARLY=2` — chip 2 only | **100.0000 %** | `[(1, 191999)]` | 0 |
| `DSP4_TX_EARLY=3` — both chips | **100.0000 %** | `[(1, 191999)]` | 0 |
| `=3` with `DSP4_GATHER_FIRST=0` | 98.2156 % | `[(1, 188573), (-31, 1713), (33, 1384), (17, 329)]` | chip 2 **2.7 %** |

The control's histogram is the defect in one line: **exactly 12,000
displacements of ±32 in 12,000 blocks — one frame per DMA half, displaced
by two blocks.** With the switch on there is **one entry in the histogram**.

**`DSP4_GATHER_FIRST` is still needed.** Turning it off with `TX_EARLY=3`
costs 1.78 points of order and 2.7 % of chip 2's blocks. The two are not
alternatives; the gather's position inside the loop is a separate deadline
from the half the loop writes into. (The 2.7 % overrun in that arm is
unexplained and is a confound on the 98.2156 %: a missed block re-transmits
a stale half, so some of that residual may be the overrun rather than the
order. Recorded rather than smoothed over.)

## 4. What it costs

### Cycles: none

`capacity.sh`, D24 mask, 45 s, 135,040 blocks per chip:

| | chip 1 `_proc_cyc` | chip 1 worst | chip 2 `_proc_cyc` | chip 2 worst | OVERRUN |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| control | 234,821 | 277,589 | 302,971 | 303,954 | 0 |
| `DSP4_TX_EARLY=3` | 234,867 | 277,760 | 304,390 | 304,882 | 0 |

Every difference is inside the pass-to-pass spread the same instrument
showed on the shipping image (303,891–304,245 on chip 2). **0.20 % of
budget was costed; 0.0 % is measured.**

### DM: none

No staging block, no third buffer.

### Latency: +16 samples per chip, seen

20 reps per arm against the `_pisel` LOGIC loop at min 14,429 / median
14,436:

| arm | min | median | DSP contribution |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| `TX_EARLY=0` | 14,496 | 14,501 | 65–67 samples |
| `TX_EARLY=2` | 14,511–14,515 | 14,518 | **82** (+16) |
| `TX_EARLY=3` | 14,526 | 14,535 | **99** (+32) |

**+16 samples / 0.333 ms per chip, on the part, not asserted.**

## 5. The ruling PW is being asked for

| option | ordered on the wire | cycles | DM | latency | what is still wrong |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **do nothing** | 87.4999 % at full D24, 81.2499 % with the Pi input | — | — | 66 samples | one frame in sixteen carries a two-block-old sample on every converter lane |
| **`DSP4_TX_EARLY=2`** | **100.0000 %**, measured | **0** | **0** | 82 samples, **+0.333 ms** | chip 1's inter-chip TX still has the same defect; the stamp cannot see it (the instrument reads chip 2's transmit path only, and LOGIC captures only `o_dspb[3]`) |
| **`DSP4_TX_EARLY=3`** | **100.0000 %**, measured | **0** | **0** | 99 samples, **+0.667 ms** | nothing known |
| Option B, third TX buffer | not built | ~0 | one more half | the same | re-derives the ping/pong phase; desynchronises RX from TX |

**Recommendation: `DSP4_TX_EARLY=3`.** It costs nothing but latency, and
the extra 0.333 ms over the chip-2-only option buys the one path that this
bench cannot witness and therefore cannot clear. `=2` is the option to take
if 0.333 ms is the whole budget; it is measurably correct on the lane the
product's converters see, and knowingly leaves chip 1's inter-chip TX
carrying the defect.

**Neither changes the SPI contract.** Both change the **published
through-DSP latency**, so the alignment contract and the window note reissue
with the new figure whichever way this goes — and they must reissue anyway,
because the block-16 figure is 66 samples and the contract still carries
72 from a block-8 image.
