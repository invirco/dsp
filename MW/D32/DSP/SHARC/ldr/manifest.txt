WITHDRAWN 2026-08-21: neither chip1.b4090de01d5d.ldr nor
chip2.bb2b24db8617.ldr is bootable, and the reason first recorded here
(a block-size ceiling) was WRONG. Both are unbootable because elfloader
compressed their zero runs into ZERO-FILL blocks, and the SPI target boot
kernel loses stream sync at the first fill block that has anything after
it — so the host clocks the whole stream out, reports success, and the
part never executes an instruction. Do not flash these, and do not treat
"boot reported OK" against them as evidence of anything.

The earlier `-MaxBlockSize` theory is retired: an A/B of the same DXE,
capped vs uncapped, 8 boots each, scored 7/8 and 6/8 — indistinguishable.
That reading came from a ~50% per-attempt failure rate caused by the boot
bus's second master, not by block size.

FIX, in the tree since 2026-08-21: `-NoFillBlock` in build.sh's LDRFLAGS,
guarded by `tools/dsp/ldr_stream.py check` on every image the build
produces. Proved at the bench, chip 2, gap-synced 11 MHz: the firmware as
elfloader emitted it booted 0/6; the same firmware with -NoFillBlock
booted 5/6, with the DSP4_BISECT=5 park firing — the first time any full
D32 firmware image has executed an instruction on this card.
