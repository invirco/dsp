The two instruments disagree at the point that matters, and both are reported. The pass-rate instrument scores 32 strips at block 32 and 983.04 MHz at 1500 of 1500 passes per second, on two separate boots. The cycle instrument puts the same graph 1,722 cycles over its budget — 0.26 %, which is under four blocks a second and beneath the pass-rate counter's resolution. So 32 channels on one 21564 at block 32 is on the line: reached by one instrument, fractionally over by the other, and not the comfortable fit a product decision would want. The previous session's best was 12.4 % over.

Two things the ceilings say on their own. D24's 24 channels now fit one chip at 786.432 MHz and block 32 (24 = 1500/s, 25 = 1458/s), which an earlier session recorded as not fitting. And a two-chip D32 split needs 16 per chip, which is under every ceiling in the table.

Why signal matters

Both dynamics nodes return early on a zero envelope, before reaching log2. The bench has no audio source, so every earlier profile measured the cheap path — GATE read 152 and COMP 161. Under signal they are 247.5 and 421.6.

The 2.39× is superseded

SIMD duplicates the compute units and register file, not the address generators, so DLY and RTG stay scalar. But 2.39× was measured against the old block cascade. Against the fused one, re-measured 29 Aug: biquad 1.43–1.54×, GATE 2.27×, COMP 2.04× as kernels, and 1.8× / 1.7× once the pair driver's gather, scatter and sample-0 work are inside the timed span. The calculator below still uses 2.39× and therefore flatters pairing.

The dynamics rework

GATE computes a log only to compare against a threshold — move the compare to the linear domain and it costs nothing. COMP needs the log value, so it needs a table. Both change fixed_ref and need D5 sign-off.

Not yet measured

Chip 2's existing master load — 235 nodes of limiters, GEQ, FX, outputs — is not in this model. A two-chip row assumes both chips start empty, which chip 2 does not. TUBE is measured bypassed; its active cost is unknown.

Family swap won't help memory

Every 2156x part carries the same 640 KB L1, and the 21564 already has the largest L2 at 1,760 KB. Only clock speed could differ, and that needs the datasheet. Dual-SHARC parts exist in 2157x/2158x/2159x but add an ARM core.
