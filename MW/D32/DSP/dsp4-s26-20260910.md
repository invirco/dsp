## 1. Gate 1 — the `fix` sweep

### 1.1 What S25 left, and why a bar could not have found it

S25 measured `fix` on this core and found it **rounds to nearest, ties to
even**. Three places in the tree said it truncates toward zero. That is a
correctness claim about every float→fixed conversion in the firmware, and
the reason no golden bar had caught it is structural: the goldens are
generated by the same host models the part is compared against, so a model
that shares the part's mistake — or, worse, differs from it on values no
bar exercises — reads as a pass.

So the instrument for gate 1 is not a bar. It is an **enumeration**, and it
is checked in as `tools/dsp/fix_sweep.py`.

### 1.2 The arithmetic the whole sweep rests on

Every float→fixed conversion in this tree has one shape:

    f_scaled = f_param * SCALE      (a float32 multiply)
    r        = fix f_scaled         (the conversion under audit)

A float32 is an exact integer whenever its magnitude is at least 2²³,
because a 24-bit significand has no bits left below the binary point. So
`fix` can only round when `|f_scaled| < 2²³`, and where SCALE is a power of
two the multiply is exact — it moves the exponent and leaves the
significand alone — which makes the `fix` the **only** rounding in the
chain. Each class therefore has an exactness **floor**:

| scale | format | exact for | in the parameter's own units |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2²⁸ | Q4.28 gain | \|g\| ≥ 2⁻⁵ | 0.03125, i.e. −30.1 dB |
| 2³¹ | Q0.31 | \|a\| ≥ 2⁻⁸ | 0.00390625 |
| 2²⁷ | Q5.27 | \|v\| ≥ 2⁻⁴ | 0.0625 |
| 2²⁵ | Q6.25 log2 | \|v\| ≥ 2⁻² | 0.25, i.e. **1.505 dB** |

Below its floor a conversion rounds, by at most half an LSB of the target
format — 2⁻²⁹ of full scale at Q4.28, which is 174 dB down. **That is the
honest size of this whole class: it moves stored words, it does not move
audio.** What matters is that the host models agree with the part word for
word, because a model that disagrees turns every future bit-exact bar into
a false alarm or a false pass.

Three constants in the tree are **not** powers of two — 126.0 (the pan
index), 2³¹/100 (the parallel-blend percentage) and the dB→log2 Q6.25
constant — so those sites are rounded twice, once by the multiply and once
by the `fix`, and a host model of one of them has to reproduce both.

### 1.3 The sweep

    $ python3 tools/dsp/fix_sweep.py --summary
    60 conversions swept, 55 exposed by one LSB below their floor,
    5 per-sample signal conversions, 10 classes

Sixty **emission sites**, not sixty files: `chip*/nodes/` is generated, so
auditing its 439 files would count one emitter hundreds of times and would
audit the output instead of the source. The sixty are the 26 in
hand-maintained assembly and the 34 in `dsp_codegen.py`.

| class | sites | what it is |
|---|--:|---|
| Q4.28 gain (× 2²⁸) | 20 | every gain, send and coefficient store |
| Q0.31 (× 2³¹) | 14 | attack/release, slope, the crossfade alpha |
| Q6.25 log2 (× 2²⁵) | 8 | thresholds and knees in the log domain |
| signal (BY form) | 5 | per-sample, where rounding is the spec |
| Q5.27 (× 2²⁷) | 3 | the biquad's halved n1 |
| pan index (× 126.0) | 2 | S25's class |
| ms → samples (× 48.0) | 2 | SPI ramp times |
| percent → Q0.31 | 2 | the compressor's parallel blend |
| soft-knee half | 2 | knee × dB→log2 × 0.5 |
| ceil intent (× 6.0) | 1 | `bq_headroom`'s prefix length |

**The two classes worth naming, because their floors sit inside ordinary
operating ranges and not in a corner:**

- **Thresholds and knees within ±1.5 dB of zero.** The Q6.25 log2 floor is
  0.25 in log2 units, which is 1.505 dB. A threshold set at −0.5 dB is
  below the floor and rounds. One LSB there is 2⁻²⁵ log2 units = 1.8 × 10⁻⁷ dB.
- **Every soft-knee width below 3.01 dB**, which is to say every ordinary
  one. `halfk = knee_dB × DB2L2Q25 × 0.5` is rounded **three** times — both
  multiplies and the `fix` — and its effective scale of 2 786 635.25 puts
  the floor at 3.01 dB.

### 1.4 The comments that were wrong, and one that was wrong twice

**`lib/bq_headroom.asm:367`** said `/* truncates; the clamp makes it moot */`.
It does not truncate. The line wants `ceil(6/umin)` and `fix` is neither
ceil nor floor, so it has never computed what it says — and it would not
have under truncation either, so nothing changes but the statement. The
"moot" half is now argued rather than asserted: the result is clamped to
[128, 1024], so only a `umin` in (0.00586, 0.0469) can see the difference
at all, and there it is at most one sample of prefix length out of at least
128.

**`num_selftest.asm` and the generator that emits it** said
`/* alpha_q31; `fix` saturates */`. **`fix` does not saturate — it wraps**,
and this tree already knew: the compressor's parallel-blend clamp exists
because a 100 % setting scales to exactly 2³¹ and `fix` returned −1 (bench
2026-08-23), and `fixed_ref` records the same measurement independently.
Two contradictory claims about one instruction, in one tree, both written
down. The comment is corrected and now states both halves — the rounding
rule and the wrap — and says which is reachable.

### 1.5 The host model that was wrong twice over, and passed anyway

`fixed_ref.xfade_alpha_q` modelled the kernel's `r4 = fix f4` as
`sat32(int(...))` — **truncation** toward zero and **saturation** on
overflow. The part does neither.

It agreed with the part on every vector, and the reason is worth keeping
because it is not a reason to trust. Over the ramp's entire reachable
domain — `alpha = k/576`, k = 0…575 — the float32 product `alpha × 2³¹` is
an **exact integer for every k except 1 and 2**:

| k | alpha × 2³¹ | fraction | truncate | ties-to-even |
|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| 1 | 3 728 270.25 | .25 | 3 728 270 | 3 728 270 |
| 2 | 7 456 540.5 | .5, even | 7 456 540 | 7 456 540 |
| all others | integer | — | equal | equal |

A quarter rounds down under both rules, and a half with an **even** integer
part rounds down under ties-to-even too. So 576 of 576 steps matched a
model that was wrong twice, and **no vector the ramp can produce would ever
have shown it.** This is S24's five-powers-of-two shape exactly, one
subsystem along.

The model now calls `fixed_ref.fix32`, which states the part's rule and
**raises** out of range rather than inventing an overflow behaviour from
the single point that has been measured. Golden harness: **59/59, no
golden moved.**

### 1.6 The witness on the part

`fix_sweep.py --check` compares each host model against the part's rule
over the domain the model is actually reachable on:

| class | host model | agree | disagree | domain |
|---|---|--:|--:|---|
| pan index | `pan_table._fix` | 127 | 0 | 127 indices |
| crossfade alpha | `fixed_ref.xfade_alpha_q` | 576 | 0 | 576 ramp steps, 2 fractional |
| Q4.28 gain, sub-floor | `fixed_ref.to_q` | 20 000 | 0 | 20 000 gains below 2⁻⁵ |

That is host-against-rule. **Host-against-part** is a new arm in
`num_selftest.asm`, driven by `numverify.sh`. One vector set answers the
question for all ten classes at once, because every conversion reaches
`fix` as a float32 that some multiply has already produced: hand the part
the float32 directly and read back the integer, with no multiply in the way
of the reading.

The table separates the three candidate rules rather than merely
exercising one — a fraction of .25 is the arm's own negative control
(every rule agrees), .75 separates truncation from both round rules, .5
with an even integer part separates ties-to-even from half-away, and .5
with an odd one separates ties-to-even from truncation. Each again with
the sign flipped, because truncation is toward **zero** and floor is not.
It includes the three products S25's pan probe actually failed on — 31.5,
63.5, 94.5 — and four out-of-range values that are **recorded, not
scored**, so the overflow rule becomes measured data instead of an
inference from one point.

## 2. Gate 2 — chip 1's code pool

### 2.1 Where the bytes are

`dsp_codepool.py --by class` on chip 1, and `shared_kernel_survey.py`'s
verdict beside it:

| class | n | each | total | % pool | shareable? |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|---|
| RTG | 32 | 1 700 | 54 400 | 22.0 % | no — 32 distinct bodies |
| **GATE** | 32 | 1 072 | 34 304 | 13.9 % | **yes** |
| **FILT** | 32 | 968 | 30 976 | 12.5 % | **yes** |
| EQ | 32 | 664 | 21 248 | 8.6 % | yes |
| FDR | 32 | 550 | 17 600 | 7.1 % | yes |
| DLY | 32 | 512 | 16 384 | 6.6 % | no — 9 distinct bodies |
| GAIN | 32 | 198 | 6 336 | 2.6 % | no — 32 distinct bodies |
| COMP / TUBE | 32 | 72 | 2 304 | 0.9 % | **already shared** (S18/S21) |

COMP and TUBE are already down to 72 bytes an instance, which is what the
pattern does when it fits. GATE and FILT are the two largest bodies it
still fits, so they are the two this session took.

### 2.2 What sharing GATE and FILT needed

The S18 machinery generalises — `SHARED_KERNEL_CLASSES` is a table and
`_shk_record` derives each record's shape from the node's own `.var`
declarations — but neither class dropped in unchanged, and both times the
generator's own premise checks said so rather than emitting something that
would have linked and misbehaved.

**FILT carries a private subroutine.** `_filt_start_xfade_<nid>` stages the
dormant coefficient set and lives in the same PM section as the body.
Rules 1–4 of the rewrite had already made its innards record-relative;
only its **name** was per-node, and 32 copies of one routine is the same
waste as 32 copies of the body that calls it. The rewrite now renames
node-local subroutines too — and **only symbols the body itself defines**,
which is the whole safety of the step: a `_<name>_<nid>` that is merely
referenced belongs to another node, and is left to fail the check rather
than be silently bound.

**FILT names its block-pool slot twice more.** The i3/i4 prologue moves
wholesale into the stub, which was enough for COMP, TUBE and GATE. FILT
also does `i2 = BLK_CHAIN_B_P1;` twice further down, and a shared body
cannot name that: **the `_P1` suffix is the strip's parity**, so baking
strip 1's form into one shared body would hand every even strip the odd
bank. The stub now loads that slot — in its own parity, read off its own
body, never the canonical node's — into a register the class does not
otherwise touch, and the body reads the register. Exactly one distinct slot
may remain; two would need a decision about which is which, and guessing
is how a node comes to write the wrong half of the pool.

That register is loaded **only into the entry that reads it**. The
per-sample entry is on the hot path — 32 strips at 48 kHz — so a stub
loading a register that half never reads is not tidy-looking waste, it is
about 3 million instructions a second.

### 2.3 Two tool defects the attempt exposed, both real

Neither was in the firmware. Both were in the instruments that guard it,
and both had been invisible while COMP and TUBE were the only shared
classes.

**`build.sh` told the layout check four of its ninety flags.**
`shared_kernel_check.py` evaluates the record's `#if`s itself, so a flag it
is not given is a flag it assumes is 0. COMP's and TUBE's records happen to
be conditional on exactly the four that were passed. GATE's and FILT's are
also conditional on `DSP4_BQ_FLOAT`, `DSP4_BQ_GUARD` and
`DSP4_PAIRED_GRAPH`, and without those the check computed offsets for a
record shape the build never assembled. `build.sh` now passes **every**
`DSP4_*` flag it holds, which closes the class rather than the instance.

**`shared_kernel_check.py` treated `#elif` as `#else`.** It inverted the
current arm without evaluating the `#elif`'s own condition. FILT's record
is `#if DSP4_BQ_FLOAT … #elif DSP4_BQ_GUARD … #else … #endif`, and with
both flags 0 the walker skipped the first arm, then **took the second one
without checking it** — counting `_filt_hrl` and `_filt_hrw` into the
record and reporting that the linker had misplaced every field after them.
1 152 failures, all its own. The generator was never wrong here: it emits
its offset `#define`s inside the same conditionals and the **assembler**
evaluates them; only this independent re-reading was wrong.

## 3. Gate 3 — the talkback and noise destinations

**Nothing was built, and that is the finding rather than a shortfall.**

The dispatch asked for a crosspoint per destination *the cell master's
enumeration actually names*, and for destinations it does not name to be
carried as the S25-5 question rather than invented. Going to build them
established that the master names **no destination at all** — only a count
— and that the count is itself contradicted by the product.

**What the master has.** `Talk[1-1]Dest[1-3]`, `Talk[2-2]Dest[1-3]`,
`Talk[3-4]Dest[1-3]` and `Noise[1-1]Dest[1-10]`, with notes reading
"Talkback routing to aux/main" and "Noise routing to aux/main outputs".
No `Table` column on any of them — and in this master every genuinely named
selector has one (`Aux[1-16]Pan[1-1]` reads `Pan:dB:0:Off`). The two halves
of the family do not agree on their own encoding either: Talk's `MxDatS` is
1, the per-slot flag shape that `Chan[1-64]MuteGrp[1-8]` uses, while
Noise's is 11, which is a selector.

**The count is contradicted by the product.**
`defs/products/d24/d24-skin-cell-map.csv`, row `menu5/2`, records against
"Talkback Assign A1-A8 + L-R":

> `RANGE CONFLICT: vocab Talk Rtg[1-3] vs 9 targets — extend to [1-9]`

So building three crosspoints per talkback mic would have built the wrong
**number** of them, on top of guessing which buses.

**The noise half is blocked by a ruling, not a gap.** Rows `menu5/6` and
`menu5/8` of the same file carry no cell at all and record PW's decision of
2026-09-06: *"Rtg retired from all cells — Noise assign needs a NEW
function definition in the master; never reuse Rtg"*. These ten `Dest`
cells are therefore not the vehicle the product is waiting for; giving them
a fan-out would build against a definition PW has already said must be
replaced.

`dsp-unmapped.csv` moves by **zero** cells, which is the correct answer
when nothing is built. What changed is the **reasons**, which now carry all
of the above at their source in `gen_dsp.py` so the next session does not
re-derive it.

The underlying deficiency S25 named is unchanged and still true:
`C1_TALK_01`, `C1_TALK_02` and `C1_NOISE` have empty outputs columns,
`_talk_route_<nid>[3]` is written by the SPI dispatch and read by nothing,
and the mics and the generator are gained, high-passed, and delivered
nowhere.
