# The 29.6 % lever, settled: `DSP4_SIMD_DYN` is audio-correct; `DSP4_C2_BQ_GRAPH` is not

**Session 12, 2026-09-09. Rev C unit at `app@192.168.1.219`, shipping CPLD
`a1f6672af6c3`, `dsp4-pcm-slave` overlay, block 16, 983.04 MHz.**

**PW ruled during this session (14:3x): S9-2 Option A is ADOPTED on
chip 2.** `DSP4_TX_EARLY=2` is now named in `shipping.config`
unconditionally, `DSP4_GATHER_FIRST` stays on beside it, block 16 is ruled
the shipping block size, and the through-DSP contract figure is **82
samples / 1.708 ms**. The tree's default pair therefore changes bytes:
`a95fd8eb…`/`fb1eee67…` becomes **`0f8add1f…`/`96ea49bf…`**, and
`DIAG_BUILD_CFG2` becomes **`0xC2010244`**.

## The one-line answer

**`DSP4_SIMD_DYN` IS audio-correct on the part.** With
`DSP4_STRIP_FUSED=1 DSP4_SIMD_DYN=1 DSP4_C2_BQ_GRAPH=0` the family walk
reads **17 of 20 families LIVE, contract 20/20 with 0 FAILED, and
COMPRESSOR / FADER_PAN / TUBE_SAT BIT_EXACT** — verdict for verdict what
the shipping pair reads, GATE's numeric NO_STIMULUS included.

The four families that read NO_CAPTURE in S11, and the GATE and numeric
arms that came back inert or unstimulated, were **three separate defects in
the instrument** and one in the graph. The instrument defects are fixed and
the fixes are inert for every unpaired build (the default pair still builds
`a95fd8eb…` / `fb1eee67…`). What is left is real and it is narrow:

**`DSP4_C2_BQ_PAIRED_GRAPH` — chip 2's paired AUX biquads — loses
parameter changes.** A band gain written into a paired AUX GEQ and a notch
gain written into a paired AUX AFB do not reach the audio. That switch is
derived from `DSP4_C2_BQ_GRAPH`, which has its own control position, so
the lever splits cleanly into a part that can ship and a part that cannot
yet.

## Gate 1 — the witness links beside the paired kernels

`DSP4_SCOPE_BLK_TAP=1` alongside `STRIP_FUSED` + `SIMD_DYN` failed to link.
Read out of the map, not believed:

| region | candidate, no tap | candidate + tap |
|---|---|---|
| Block 3 `mem_block3_bw` (code) | `0x1fffe` used, **2 bytes free** | full |
| Block 2 `mem_block2_bw` (code overflow) | `0x1ecb4` used, `0x134c` free | full |
| Block 1 `mem_block1_bw` (DM ovf + DMA) | `0x12c08` used, **`0x193f8` free** | `0x13246` used |

The witness costs **+2,976 words** in the generated chain (a `_scope_tap`
call site per node) and **+155** in `scope.asm`. The linker reported
`0x63e` words unmapped once for `sec_swco` and once for `sec_swco_ovf` —
the same remainder twice, so the shortfall is **799 words / 1,598 bytes**
against 103 KB of Block 1 the LDF was never allowed to reach.

`ADSP-21564.ldf` gains `sec_swco_ovf2 > mem_block1_bw`, declared **after**
`sec_dmda_ovf`, `sec_dmda_c_bw_ovf` and `sec_dma`, so DM and the DMA
ping-pong buffers take Block 1 first and code takes only the leftover. The
chip-1 candidate places **`0x31f` words** there; chip 2 never reaches it.

Two things are stated rather than assumed. **It is inert when not needed:**
the default pair rebuilt across the change is byte-identical
(`a95fd8eb…`/`fb1eee67…`). **It costs timing where it is used:**
instruction fetch from Block 1 runs against DDE traffic, so no capacity
number in this session is taken from a build that reaches that section.
The audio path under test is not changed by any of it — the same objects,
the same code, 1,598 bytes of it at a different address.

Every arm below is `famverify.sh` with the tap on, on the same bench, on
2026-09-09, `PRODUCT=d24`, each from its own staging path under
`/home/app/dspfam/`, each with a fresh `sym.json` from its own build.

The shipping pair `blk_chip1.ldr`/`blk_chip2.ldr` (`ac65ad38…`/`e5dce9e4…`)
was **reproduced byte for byte** from a worktree at `dcb7d1f` before any of
this, so the shipping arm is the shipping bytes' own configuration and not
a near neighbour of it.

| arm | image | LIVE | contract | COMP / FDR / TUBE numeric |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| shipping (`blk_*` configuration + tap) | `25f0a532…`/`9d713603…` | **17/20** | 20/20 | **BIT_EXACT** |
| `STRIP_FUSED` + `SIMD_DYN`, tap, **as S11 left the instrument** | `0b83a8ed…`/`fa6e8848…` | 12/20 | 20/20 | NO_STIMULUS |
| the same image, witness fixed (S12-2/3/4) | `f7450174…`/`26bdd148…` | 15/20 | 20/20 | **BIT_EXACT** |
| **`STRIP_FUSED` + `SIMD_DYN` + `C2_BQ_GRAPH=0`**, witness fixed | `f7450174…`/`3adea11f…` | **17/20** | 20/20 | **BIT_EXACT** |

Three of the four differences were the instrument. They are written up in
full as findings S12-2, S12-3 and S12-4; in one line each:

* **The tap found pair drivers with a regex** over chip 1's four driver
  names, so chip 2's `_C2PAIR_*`/`_C2BQP_*` pairs were never tapped — 124
  taps in the paired chip-2 chain against 200 in the scalar one — and the
  four families the scope was armed on **timed out**: NO_CAPTURE. The
  emitters now record their own members.
* **A pair driver does not always publish where its class's scalar kernel
  does.** `_DYNGATE` leaves channel A in `BLK_CHAIN_B_P1`; the scalar GATE
  leaves it in `BLK_CHAIN_A_P1`. The tap was copying the block the gate had
  *read* — GATE INERT, peak exactly the injected `0x08000000` on both sides
  of the step. COMP, FILT and EQ hid it because for them the two addresses
  coincide.
* **The numeric arm injected into `_blk_pool`** on a build where strip 1,
  being odd, runs on `_blk_pool1`. Nothing on the strip moved
  (`_buf_C1_IN_01` … `_buf_C1_FDR_01` all peak `0x00000001`) and three
  families reported NO_STIMULUS on an image whose audio arm read them LIVE.

### The fourth difference is the audio, and it is one switch

With the instrument fixed, exactly two families still differ from
shipping, and turning **`DSP4_C2_BQ_GRAPH=0`** on the *same image* brings
both back. Same bench, same day, same `PRODUCT=d24`, same witness address:

| family | witness | chip-2 biquads PAIRED | chip-2 biquads SCALAR |
|---|---|---|---|
| GEQ | `_buf_C2_AUX_GEQ_01` | peak `0x08000000` → `0x08000000`, **moved 0 of 64** → INERT | peak `0x08000000` → `0x082DE520`, moved 64 of 64 → LIVE |
| ANTI_FB | `_buf_C2_AUX_AFB_01` | peak `0x08000000` → `0x08000000`, **moved 0 of 64** → INERT | peak `0x08000000` → `0x07FB92D0`, moved 64 of 64 → LIVE |
| CROSSOVER | `_buf_C2_MAIN_OEQ_01` | peak `0x07E960D0` → `0x074AF178`, moved 64 of 64 → LIVE | the same numbers |

**This is the audio, not the witness.** The address is the node's own
`_blk_<nid>` block array in both arms; the unpaired arm proves that address
is right; and `_C2BQP_MOUT_OEQ_01_02` moves its block on the paired image,
so the paired path can publish. The injected step comes out of a paired
AUX GEQ and a paired AUX AFB **bit-unchanged in both the low and the high
coefficient state**: the parameter write never reaches the arithmetic.

The mechanism is not settled on the part, and two are consistent with
what was measured. The chip-2 pair "owns the interleaved coefficient and
state arrays and LATCHES them", gathering once when the pair engages and
scattering back when it disengages: either **the latch does not come down
for these two parameter paths** (MAIN_OEQ's probe writes a crossover
frequency, which evidently does take it down), or **the two nodes do not
run at all in that chain** and the block the witness reads is simply the
one the previous node left. Both are the audio and both are disqualifying;
which it is decides the size of the fix. Settling it is the next session's
work **if** the chip-2 biquad pairing is wanted for capacity — which is a
capacity question, and the capacity answer is below.

## Method, and the two traps this session walked into

Every image in this note is built from `shipping.config` plus named
overrides, staged to its own path, booted with `dsp4_boot.py` behind the
chip-identity gate, and read through a `sym.json` generated from that
build's own map. The default pair is rebuilt after every source change and
its md5 quoted, because "inert" is a claim about bytes.

**Trap one: naming a switch in `shipping.config` changes every build's
default.** `DSP4_C2_BQ_GRAPH=0` was added to that file part-way through the
session, and the next capacity arm labelled "chip-2 biquads PAIRED" built
the unpaired image — silently, because the arm's overrides list printed
only what the environment named. It was caught by the `.ldr` md5 in the
arm's own log, which is why the md5 is printed. A control arm must now name
the opposite value explicitly.

**Trap two: a bench measurement is not the bar it looks like.** Four of the
six design bars arm the scope on `_buf_<node>`, which under block kernels
is a one-word variable the kernel never writes. Run straight, `fxverify`
reports the shipping pair's FX input as **silent** and refuses a verdict —
not a failure, a bar reading the wrong address. See S12-8; all six bars in
this note were run on tap-enabled images.

**The two halves of the lever pull in opposite directions.** candA
reproduces S11 to within half a percent — D32 fits, chip 2 at 82 % of
budget with **zero** missed blocks over 135,041 on both boots, worst block
87.1 % against S11's 86.75 — and it is the configuration whose aux GEQ and
aux AFB do not respond to a parameter change. candB is audio-clean and
**does not fit D32**: chip 2 runs 2.4–5.7 % of blocks over, the shipping
pair's failure mode at D32, smaller.

**Chip 2's biquad pairing is the difference, and it is most of the lever:**
20.3 % of budget at D32 (102.5 → 82.1) and 16.3 % at D24 (83.3 → 67.6).
`STRIP_FUSED` + the paired *dynamics* + the pair-ordered chain — everything
that is proven — buy chip 2 the rest, which at D32 is not enough on its own.

**Option A is free on the part, and that is measured rather than assumed.**
`candS12` is `candB` plus `DSP4_TX_EARLY=2`, and chip 2 reads **102.69 %**
against candB's **102.48 %** at D32 — inside the boot-to-boot spread of the
same arm — with the same 2.37 % overrun. The part reads back
`cfg2 0xC201024F … tx_early 2`, so the ruling is visible in the image and
not only in the file.

**At D24 both fit with more room than the pair that ships**: chip 1 59.3 %
average against the shipping pair's 71.5 % and 72.4 % worst against 84.8 %,
chip 2 83–87 % (candB) or 67.6 % (candA) against 92.7 %.

Two things are stated rather than smoothed over.

**The `_proc_cyc_max` reading is not usable on chip 2 in these runs.** On
two of eight chip-2 boots it reads 382 % and 406 % of budget while
`OVERRUN` reads 0 and the average reads 82–87 %; the other six read
71–103 % and track the average. A single block of 1.25 M cycles cannot be
both true and invisible to the overrun flag, so one of the two instruments
is wrong about the same blocks. The verdicts above are taken from the
**average and the overrun count**, which agree with each other, with the
second boot of each arm, and with S10's arithmetic. S10-8's rule that the
worst block is the figure the budget must cover still stands for chip 1,
where max and average track on every boot.

**Both arms read back the same `DIAG_BUILD_CFG2`, `0xC201004F`** — the word
does not carry `DSP4_C2_BQ_GRAPH`, so an image that loses parameter changes
and an image that does not are indistinguishable to the part. That is
S12-7, and it is why the switch is named in `shipping.config` now.

## Gate 3 — the six design bars, STAGE-safe, on both images

All six now take `STAGE` and none of them can scp over `~/dspboot/chip[12].ldr`
again (S10-7). `busgold` shares `pairgraph_run.sh` with three scripts out of
scope, so that run half takes `STAGE` too and the other three callers, which
never set it, are unchanged.

Running them produced a result the gate did not ask for and the record needs:
**four of the six cannot certify anything on a block-kernel image today.**
Not one of them set `DSP4_SCOPE_BLK_TAP`; S11-5 found exactly this in
`famverify.sh` and the fix was never carried across (S12-8). Every arm below
is on a tap-enabled build of the configuration named, and the four scripts
now **default the tap ON**, so the next person to run one straight does not
get the answer this session started with. It does not rescue
`xoververify`, which stalls for a different reason.

| bar | `blk_*` (shipping) | candB (the candidate) | candA (`C2_BQ_GRAPH=1`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `fxverify` | **FX_VERIFY_OK** | **FX_VERIFY_OK** | — |
| `busgold` | **GRAPH BIT-EXACT** (shipping switches) | **GRAPH BIT-EXACT** (`FUSED=1 SIMD=1`) | — |
| `afbverify` | design PASS 4 ulp; live tone FAIL | identical, to every digit | **coeff bank IDENTITY, 83,887,018 ulp; tone +0.000 dB** |
| `geqverify` | design PASS 1–3 ulp; live tone FAIL | identical, to every digit | **coeff bank IDENTITY, 50,965,640 ulp; tone +0.000 dB** |
| `xoververify` | **stalls**, 0 of 1024 samples | stalls, identically | — |
| `bqeverify` | **BQE_VERIFY PASS**, both round-once arms — builds its own arm, already `FUSED=1 SIMD=1` | | |

`busgold` reads **bit-exact on both**, capture sha256 `ba3f52ecb83f9a60`
either way, with `paired_build=False` on one arm and `True` on the other —
so the bus-accumulation graph is unchanged by the candidate. The bar's own
two warnings travel with that: both captures are **bypass biquads** (bit
identical paired or not) and **unity gain** (Q4.28 `2^28`, so every low bit
is zero and nothing can saturate). It is a real pass on the bus graph and a
weak one on everything the paired path changes.

`bqeverify` is the one bar whose own build is already the candidate's
switch positions (`DSP4_STRIP_FUSED=1 DSP4_SIMD_DYN=1`), and it **passes on
both arms**: `_bq_fx_cascade_simd` — the PAIRED cascade kernel — hashes
`0x7B71525D`/`0x0A6F911E` against the contract model and
`0x43E57EE8`/`0xEA1129D6` against round-once, MATCH each time over 36,864
words, with the A-vs-B divergence reproducing the model's prediction
exactly (1,797 differing words, first at 1366, max |d| 2147438683, and the
same 30 of 576 overflow cells). **So the paired biquad ARITHMETIC is
bit-exact to the model.** Taken with the coefficient-bank result below,
that puts the chip-2 fault squarely in coefficient DELIVERY and not in the
SIMD cascade.

`xoververify` arms on `_buf_lp_<nid>` — an address the block tap does not
cover, because the tap knows node identities and not crossover leg buffers.
It is S12-2's defect class again, in a bar rather than in the codegen.

`afbverify` and `geqverify` did the most useful work of the six, and it was
not the work they were queued for. Both **reproduce bit-for-bit across
`blk_*` and candB** — the aux biquads are scalar in both, so it is the same
code — which says the measurement is deterministic. And on candA both
report the node's own coefficient bank still at **identity** after the host
wrote the parameters, with a modelled response error equal to the whole
designed filter: **−12.00000 dB** for the GEQ band, **+18.00000 dB** for the
AFB notch. Those same banks read **1–3 ulp** correct on the same image with
`C2_BQ_GRAPH=0`. That is the mechanism behind S12-5, from an instrument
that never looks at the audio: on a paired chip-2 build a band gain and a
notch gain **never become coefficients at all**. The node is called, it
runs, and it has nothing to apply.

**What still fails and is NOT attributed** (S12-9): on the scalar arms —
the shipping pair included — the live tone arm under-reads a filter whose
coefficients the same bar certifies at 1–3 ulp. A −18 dB notch measures
−4.799 dB and a +12 dB band measures +8.451 dB, and both bars' passband
points drift 0.15–0.36 dB in directions no filter change produces. The arm
is demonstrably sensitive (it reads +0.000 dB on candA), so this is not
insensitivity; leakage from a capture stitched out of block copies that are
not consecutive in time would fill a narrow null far more than it flattens
a broad boost, which is the ordering seen. It was `AFB_DESIGN_OK` on
2026-09-08, on a per-sample block-8 image. **It is the pair that ships, it
is one arm to separate, and it is not settled here.**

## Latency — not measured, and the arm is why

**PW ruled S9-2 Option A ADOPTED on chip 2 (2026-09-09 14:3x)**, so
`DSP4_TX_EARLY=2` is folded into `shipping.config` unconditionally and the
through-DSP contract figure is **82 samples / 1.708 ms at block 16**. That
is the ruling's number, carried from S11's measurement of the same switch
on the shipping configuration; **this session did not re-measure it, and
the reason is not a shortage of time.**

Both candidate latency arms — with and without `DSP4_TX_EARLY=2` — returned
`offset 14779, coherent 0.0 %, spread 0` on all 20 reps of both boots. That
is S11-6's known bad signature, with the pass-through setup demonstrably
running. **The control decides it: the same arm on the staged shipping pair
`blk_*` returns the identical 14779 / 0.0 %.** The instrument is at fault;
the candidate is uninvolved; and S11's 66 samples at `TX_EARLY=0` is not
overwritten with a null.

The cause is the capture path, not the firmware: the through-DSP arm needs
the `_maincap` bitstream **and** a duplex PCM overlay, and the bench lives
on `dsp4-pcm-slave`, where the capture device exists and returns audio that
is not the DSP's output. S11 flipped the overlay and restored it; that flip
is a `config.txt` line and a **reboot**, and it was not taken at this point
in the session with the card in a known-good state.

**One thing this did close (S12-10).** The tool's own guard warns when the
winning offset's margin over the runner-up is under 2x — but when the
runner-up is `0.0` the margin is INFINITE, so the guard is false and a run
in which nothing correlated printed a clean summary. S11-6 fixed the cause
of the signature and left the reporting hole. `dsp4_dsp_latency.py` now
refuses the verdict when the coherent fraction is 0.0 % on every rep, exits
2, and names the capture-path requirement.

**To re-measure**, in this order: flip `config.txt` to the duplex overlay
and reboot, `./loadlogic.sh maincap`, run the two arms, restore the overlay
and the shipping bitstream. The tool will now say so rather than answer.
