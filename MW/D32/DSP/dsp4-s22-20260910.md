## 0. Gate 0 — S21-7 settled: the gate state was never frozen (S22-1)

PW is being asked to sign `shipping.config.s21`, and `DSP4_SIMD_DYN` is one
of its five switches. S21-7 said that on any image with that switch on, one
strip of every gate pair has four FROZEN state words — envelope, gain,
target and hold count — while the partner strip's hold counter decrements on
every sample; that it reproduced to the digit across four builds and several
boots; that a four-way bisect put it on that switch alone; and that whether
the AUDIO differs was not established.

**The audio was NOT affected. There was no frozen state. The four words that
did not move belong to a gate that was being HELD OPEN by a step stimulus
nobody had stopped — and `DSP4_SIMD_DYN` was a proxy for which branch of the
generated call chain carries the witness the bar arms on, not a cause.**

### What S21-7 measured, and what it was

`goldnode`'s GATE arm is armed on **`_gate_gain_<nid>`** and has to be: the
node's own output is `x * gain`, which is zero wherever the stimulus is, so
neither an impulse nor a step can see the hold matter or the gate close
(S21-5 established this and moved the arm onto that word deliberately).

`_gate_gain_<nid>` is a ONE-WORD variable the block kernel writes once per
block, so the block-aware witness for it is `_scope_tap1` — one real word
per block — emitted by `dsp_codegen.py` from a per-node list called
`blk_extra`, alongside the `_scope_tap` block witnesses.

**The pass that emits those witnesses walks the finished call chain, and the
branch that handles a PAIR DRIVER call `continue`s before it ever reaches
`blk_extra`.** In the generated chip-1 chain, before this session:

```asm
#if ... /* paired: DSP4_SIMD_DYN on */
    call _DYNGATE_01_02_process;
    r0 = _buf_C1_GATE_01;  r1 = BLK_CHAIN_B_P1;  call _scope_tap;
    r0 = _buf_C1_GATE_02;  r1 = BLK_CHAIN_B;     call _scope_tap;
#elif ... /* scalar: DSP4_SIMD_DYN off */
    call _C1_GATE_01_process;
    r0 = _buf_C1_GATE_01;       r1 = BLK_CHAIN_A_P1;         call _scope_tap;
    r0 = _gate_gain_C1_GATE_01; r1 = _gate_gain_C1_GATE_01;  call _scope_tap1;
#endif
```

The `_scope_tap1` line the bar depends on exists **only on the scalar
branch**. `blk_extra` is keyed by NODE id (`C1_GATE_01`) and the pair branch
looks nothing up at all.

So on a paired image no call site ever presents the armed address.
`_scope_tap`/`_scope_tap1` both return early on `r0 != _scope_src`,
`_scope_idx` never advances, `sc.wait()` times out — and **nothing ever
clears `_scope_arm`**, because the tap is what owns disarming in a
`DSP4_SCOPE_BLK_TAP` build (`_scope_record` stands down there by design, so
it cannot disarm either). `_scope_inject_blk` is gated on `_scope_arm` alone
and `mode 2` is a STEP, so it kept rewriting the strip's whole block with the
amplitude **on every block, indefinitely**.

A gate whose input is held above threshold is OPEN, and every one of the four
words S21 called frozen is what an open, driven gate holds:

| word | value | why it does not move |
|---|--:|---|
| envelope | 221,910,951 | the injected step is 0x0D3A17B5 = 221,910,965; the envelope is **14 counts** under it, the attack ladder's own quantisation stall point — which is why it reproduced *to the digit* across four builds and several boots |
| gain | 268,435,447 | 9 counts under unity and still converging up |
| gain target | 268,435,456 | **exactly** unity — the open arm |
| hold count | 10 | the RELOAD value (`f32(0.2)` ms → 9.6 samples), rewritten every sample by the open arm |

and the partner strip, which the tool never injects, is closed with a hold
counter that runs — also by design, and the contrast that made it look like
an asymmetry between the two halves of the SIMD pair.

### The measurement that settles it

One image, one boot, `shipping.config.s21` witness arm
(`7f4417bd`/`e1673170`, `DIAG_BUILD_CFG2 0xC2019E7F`), `DSP4_SIMD_DYN` ON
throughout (`tools/pi/dsp4_gate_latch2.py`):

```
  quiet     scope arm=1 idx=0 go=1 runs=15
              s1 [env gain tgt hold] = [221910951, 268435447, 268435456, 10]
              s2 [env gain tgt hold] = [0, 2684356, 2684355, -25552752]
  wait: capture stalled at 0 of 1024 samples
  ... eight seconds, unchanged ...
now clearing _scope_arm by hand (the stimulus stops here):
  t= 0.0s   scope arm=0 idx=0 go=0 runs=16
              s1 [env gain tgt hold] = [2, 2684356, 2684355, -6464]
  t= 8.0s   s1 [env gain tgt hold] = [2, 2684356, 2684355, -398448]
```

**`idx=0 of 1024` is the mechanism stated by the instrument itself**: the
capture never took a single sample, because nothing answers for the armed
address. And the moment the stimulus is stopped, strip 1 falls to
`[2, 2684356, 2684355]` with its hold counter running — **the exact rest
state S21 recorded for the `DSP4_SIMD_DYN`-OFF arm**, on an image with the
switch ON.

The bisect was therefore measuring which branch of the call chain carries the
witness, not what the switch does to the gate: `DSP4_SIMD_DYN` selects the
paired branch, and the other three switches do not, which is exactly why all
three of them reproduced the "freeze".

### Two further corrections to S21-7

- **`_gate_pair_blk` DOES write its state back**, to both members. S21's
  corroboration ("`_DYNGATE_nn_mm_process` has no write-back at all") read
  the driver WRAPPER; the scatter is in the kernel, `lcntr = 4` over
  `_gat_st` into each node's own four words, and it is *more* complete than
  `_comp_pair_blk`'s, which scatters one word and leaves the gain display to
  the wrapper's `_cmp_gn` copy. Probed live (`dsp4_gate_pair_probe.py`):
  both the PEx and the PEy columns of `_gat_st` move.
- **On the shipped pairs the freeze does not occur at all.** `s20_*`
  (`32dc1ea1`/`29d00a5e`) and `s21_*` (`81f799f9`/`11366344`) both put
  strips 1 AND 2 at rest with BOTH hold counters decrementing. On a non-tap
  image `_scope_inject_blk` has no chip-1 redirect, so the stimulus reaches
  nothing and no gate is ever driven — the freeze is a property of the
  witness build and cannot occur in a shipping image.

**So the audio question is closed by construction rather than by a bar: there
is no frozen state for the audio to be affected by.**

### The fix, and it moves no shipping byte

Three changes, none of them in an audio kernel and none of them in a
shipping image.

1. **`tools/dsp/dsp_codegen.py` — emit the extras on the pair branch too,
   for BOTH members.** The `blk_extra` emission is lifted into a helper and
   called from the pair-driver branch as well as the by-name branch. The
   regenerated chains are **purely additive**: chip 1 gains 128 `_scope_tap1`
   sites and chip 2 gains 26, and **not one line is removed**. All of it is
   inside `#if DSP4_BLOCK_KERNELS && DSP4_SCOPE_BLK_TAP`.

   The chip-2 half is a blind spot nobody had looked for: the paired
   dynamics on that chip — `C2_GRP_GATE_01-04`, `C2_GRP_COMP_01-04`,
   `C2_MAIN_OCOMP_01-04`, `C2_MAIN_COMP`, `C2_SUB_COMP` — had no gain-word
   witness either, so any bar armed on one of them would have stalled the
   same way and driven the graph for as long as it was left armed.

2. **`tools/pi/dsp4_node_verify.py::capture()` — disarm in a `finally`.**
   A completed run has already disarmed itself, so this is a no-op on the
   happy path and the whole of the fix on the other one. It is in the
   `finally` and not after the retry loop because a retry must start from an
   undriven graph too. This is the guard that would have turned S21-7 from a
   finding into a line of log four sessions ago.

3. **`tools/pi/dsp4_gate_latch.py`** — the instrument S21 pointed PW at now
   prints `_scope_arm` on every line and takes the watch a second time with
   the stimulus explicitly stopped. An armed scope twelve seconds after a
   run is the answer, and the tool used not to show it.

`goldnode.sh` also gained `STRIPS="1 2"` (the two strips of a pair are not
interchangeable evidence — one is the pair kernel's PEx and the other its
PEy) and now ships `dsp4_scope.py` to the bench with the run, so a bench copy
cannot silently predate the arm/disarm contract the bar depends on.

**W0 through the generator change, both halves:**

| build | md5 after the fix | expected |
|---|---|---|
| default (`shipping.config`) | `302d6142701b00e62f0d040fdd5568f5` / `3b3a6f8e1bb94851b7c8a090440e86b4` | the pair every session since S17 has produced |
| `shipping.config.s21`, tap OFF | `81f799f9fab15892e76091b92aec95ba` / `11366344c1db42b97ccc0a68c92704b3` | the STAGED `s21_*` pair |

**So `s21_*` does not change and does not need re-staging** — which is the
answer to the addendum's (d), and the reason this fix can land while PW is
signing that configuration.

### The verdict the bar could not give: both strips of a pair, bit-exact

With the tap site emitted on the paired branch, `goldnode`'s GATE arm scores
`DSP4_SIMD_DYN` for the first time. Witness arm `270303e8`/`2ec5ffbf`,
`DIAG_BUILD_CFG2 0xC2019E7F`, `STRIPS="1 2"`:

| | strip 1 (the pair kernel's PEx) | strip 2 (its PEy) |
|---|---|---|
| chain witness at `_buf_C1_EQ_nn` | `0x08000000` | `0x08000000` |
| state at rest | `[2, 2684356, 2684355]` | `[2, 2684356, 2684355]` |
| converted parameters | range floor, LINTHR threshold, attack alpha all `ok` | all `ok` |
| phase profile | 0-14: 61-62, **15: 64** | 0-14: 61-62, **15: 64** |
| impulse @ 0x0D3A17B5 | **64 of 64 bit-exact**, control differs in 2 of 64 (predicted 2) | **64 of 64 bit-exact**, control differs in 2 of 64 (predicted 2) |
| verdict | **NODE VERIFY BIT-EXACT** | **NODE VERIFY BIT-EXACT** |

Both halves of the SIMD pair reproduce `fixed_ref` word for word from the
same rest state, with the negative control firing exactly as predicted, and
the phase profile is S21-5's signature unchanged — phase 15, the end-of-block
word the tap's position predicts, is the one that scores 64 of 64.

**The gate now reaches rest on a paired image**, which it is worth saying
plainly: `state at rest: [2, 2684356, 2684355]` is the reading S21 could
obtain only with `DSP4_SIMD_DYN` OFF, and it is here with the switch ON. The
switch was never the difference.

### S22-3, found on the way: the scope could drive STRIP 1 and no other strip

Strip 2 did not fail on the first attempt with the tap fix in place — it
reported *"the injection is NOT reaching `_buf_C1_EQ_02` (captured peak 1)"*,
three amplitudes running, and the chain witness agreed: every node of strip 2
at `0x00000001` while strip 1's read `0x08000000`.

**There was exactly ONE `_scope_inject_blk` call site in the whole chain**,
emitted after chain index 0 — `C1_IN_01` — and hard-wired to
`BLK_CHAIN_A_P1`. An injection meant for strip N therefore landed before
strip N's own input kernel ran and was overwritten by it. Strip 1 worked, and
worked BY LUCK: `dsp4_node_verify.py::inject_addr()` handed back a pool BASE
(`_blk_pool1` for an odd strip), and `_blk_pool1 + 0` happens to be strip 1's
own first chain slot.

Fixed three ways, all inside `#if DSP4_SCOPE_BLK_TAP`:

- the chain emits **one injector call site per strip**, each naming its own
  RX slot in `r0` and its own live chain slot in `r1` (135 sites on chip 1,
  zero lines removed);
- `_scope_inject_blk` **returns immediately at a site whose slot the host did
  not arm on** — without that, all 32 sites would write a block at whatever
  raw address `_scope_inj` holds, which is the one-word-variable overrun
  S9-5 was about, thirty-one times over;
- `inject_addr()` names the **RX slot symbol**, which is what the S9-5
  redirect was built to resolve, and the pool arithmetic is deleted rather
  than extended. Strip 1 resolves to the same place it always did — proved by
  its verdict being unchanged.

**W0 after this change too:** the control build is still `81f799f9` /
`11366344`.

## 1. Gate 1 — the matrix mixer

### What the definition actually gives, and where it disagrees with itself

Read off the cell master (`defs/common/cells/mx_master.csv`), not off the
product def:

```
Matrix[1-4]Level[1-1]          matrix output master level
Matrix[1-4]Mute[1-1]           matrix output mute
Matrix[1-4]Name[1-1]           label; the host stores it
Chan[1-64]MatrixOn[1-2]        channel-to-matrix bus send on/off
Chan[1-64]MatrixSend[1-2]      per-channel send level to each matrix bus
```

**So the definition gives FOUR matrix output masters and a channel the means
to reach TWO of them.** Three things disagree and none of them is a dsp
decision:

- `d32.csv` says `mtx,12`. The cell master caps the family at
  `Matrix[1-4]`, so twelve is unreachable; D32 expands to four.
  `d24.csv` says `mtx,2` and expands to two, which is consistent.
- `Chan[1-64]MatrixSend[1-2]` does not track the `mtx` count the way
  `Chan[1-64]AuxSend[1-12]` tracks `aux`. On D24 (`mtx,2`) the two match; on
  D32 (four outputs) buses 3 and 4 have a master and no source.
- the topology master (`defs/common/topology/diagram-master.csv`) names
  three matrix sources — `bus.main -> bus.mtx`, `bus.grp -> bus.mtx` and
  `ctl.auxpick -> bus.mtx` — and **the cell master defines a cell for none
  of the first two**. Main-to-matrix and group-to-matrix sends are therefore
  a definition that does not exist yet, not a node that was skipped.

**Built: exactly what the cells name.** Four matrix buses, four output
strips, channel sends onto the first two. Buses 3 and 4 carry no crosspoint
— their rows of `_xpc` keep the zeros `rtg_fabric.asm` initialises, so
`_xp_lo > _xp_hi` and the accumulate skips them for one compare per block —
and their masters are addressed, because `Matrix003Level` and
`Matrix004Mute` are defined cells and the unmapped list is the completeness
record. Nothing was invented: no `Chan*MatrixPick`, no `Matrix*Mtr`, no
`Matrix*Eq`, because no product names one.

**Questions for PW, none of them answerable here:** should
`Chan*MatrixSend` follow the `mtx` count (i.e. `[1-4]` on D32)? should the
matrix take feeds from main and the groups, as the topology master says and
the cell master does not? and should a matrix send have a selectable
pickoff, as aux and fx sends do?

### The pick-off, and it is not a choice

`Chan*AuxPick` and `Chan*FxPick` exist; **`Chan*MatrixPick` does not.** So
every matrix send is POST-FADER, which is also the only pickoff the dense
bus-major path can take without parking another tap. The kernel keeps a pick
array defaulting to PostFdr purely so the send-ramp helper is identical for
all three kinds; nothing is dispatched to it.

### Where the matrix goes: the fabric, and no CPLD change

The four buses accumulate on chip 1 with the channels, and cross to chip 2
on the inter-chip mix fabric at **global slots 37-40 = MIX_2 slots 5-8**,
which were reserved. The legacy 25 keep global slots 0-24 — asserted in
`gen_dsp_csv.py`, and the matrix slots are asserted against 37-40 beside
them, so a slot-map edit that moved either is a generator failure and not a
surprise on the bench.

**This is a slot-map contract bump and NOT a bitstream change.** The `MIX_*`
lines are DSPA `O<n>` -> DSPB `I<n>` direct: `external_net` is empty for all
eight of them, MIX_2 is already `ok` and already framed TDM16, and **no
`MIXSLOT_*` constant appears anywhere in `shared/dsp4-logic/rtl/`** — the
CPLD generates the clocks and touches none of the payload. `source_hash`
moves to `sha256:3308fbf822f5fb2a…`; the flashed bitstream does not have to.

### Do the matrix outputs have lanes? Not on the DACs

The sixteen DACs are fully committed on both products — aux 1-12 on
DAC_01..12, the four post-crossover main outputs on DAC_13..16 — so **there
is no free DAC lane for a matrix output on either product.** What is free is
the NET output block: `NET_OUT_01` already carries `C2_SUB_OUT`, and
`NET_OUT_02..32` are unassigned and scoped BOTH. Matrix 1-4 are patched onto
`NET_OUT_02..05` on exactly the footing the sub output sits on — which is to
say PROVISIONALLY. Which physical connector a matrix output appears on is a
product decision; changing it later moves one `output_params()` argument and
no address.

### The scope, proved by the unmapped list

| | landed | proposed | Δ unmapped | Δ dsp.csv |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| D32 | 1590 | 1454 | **−136** | **+136** |
| D24 | 1248 | 1148 | **−100** | **+100** |

136 is 128 `Chan*MatrixSend/On` plus 8 `Matrix*Level/Mute`; 100 is 96 plus 4
on D24's 24 channels and two matrices. Rows plus unmapped is conserved on
both products, and **no matrix cell remains in either unmapped list.**

**And not one existing address moves**: 5,409 D32 cells and 3,737 D24 cells
are in both the landed and the proposed file and every one keeps its
address. That is what the second SPI block on the routing node is for —
growing the 60-word block to 64 would have moved every chip-1 address above
channel 1's routing node, the whole map, the MCU's ghost table and every
stored golden, for four words. The matrix send block is allocated after
every other chip-1 allocation and named on the routing row as
`mtx_page`/`mtx_addr`; the chip-2 matrix strips are allocated after every
other chip-2 node.

The graph is therefore AHEAD of the landed contract, which `gen_dsp.py`
reports rather than papers over: the proposal is written to
`proposals/defs/products/<p>/` for the hub gate, and this session builds from
it with `DSP_LANDED_DIR=proposals/defs/products`, which announces its own
provenance on every run.

### It builds, and what it costs where chip 1 is tight

`021e07d1` / `81d0fa04`, zero errors — and the cost that is not cycles is
the one S20-3 made the constraint:

| chip 1 | `s21_*` | + matrix | delta |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
