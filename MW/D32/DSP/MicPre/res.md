### Design Method

conductance of the next larger one. With 6 bits (0-63 weights),
this spreads 64 combinations roughly evenly over the 0-60 dB range.
Values snapped to E96 and locally refined to minimise the largest gap.

## EIN Notes

- At low gain (high Rg), EIN dominated by op-amp input noise
- At high gain (low Rg), 2N7002 Rds_on (~3R) adds Johnson noise but is small vs Rg
- Max DSP trim of 7.31 dB keeps digital gain-up minimal
- Worst EIN jump at largest analog gap (15.18 dB) — DSP bridges this
- For best EIN at high gain, prefer DSP trim slightly negative (analog above target)
