# GATE family — hardware vs fixed_ref, 2026-08-23

provenance: AI-drafted 2026-08-23 — prose may carry a statistical watermark; rewrite by hand before publication, then remove this header.

**PASS — 0 LSB at all 7 input levels.** No defects found.

## Method

Step injected at `_rx_slot_C1_IN_01`, captured at `_buf_C1_GATE_01` with
the DSP-side scope. GAIN, both FILT sections and all four EQ bands forced
to unity first, so the gate sees the injected step and nothing else.

Threshold −40 dB, range 0.001 (−60 dB floor), attack and release alpha 0.2,
hold 4 samples. Settled value at sample 299.

**Worst error 0 LSB (Q4.28).** The threshold sits exactly where it should:
−42.14 dBFS gated, −38.62 dBFS open, against a −40 dB setting.

Model is the whole node, not just the threshold law: envelope by
`fixed_ref.envelope_step`, level by `fixed_ref.log2_q`, gain target 1.0 or
`range` with the hold counter, then a one-pole gain smoother and the
output multiply — with every parameter conversion modelled in float32 as
the DSP performs it.

## Initial state matters, and it is not the initialiser

A first pass scored 6 of 7 exact with the loudest input 1 LSB low. That was
the model, not the DSP. `_gate_gain` initialises to 1.0, but by the time a
measurement runs the part has been sitting in silence for a long time, so
the gate is **closed** and the gain has settled near `range` — 268437,
which is itself 2 LSB above `rngq` because the fixed-point one-pole sticks
once its increment rounds to zero. Reopening from there lands 1 LSB short
of unity, which is exactly what the hardware returned.

Modelling from the settled closed state rather than the declared
initialiser took the worst error to 0. Same lesson as the compressor
envelope: **a fixed-point one-pole does not reach its target**, and
assuming it does manufactures a hardware fault out of nothing.

## Note, not a defect

`_comp_lim_mode` is declared in every COMP node and never referenced by the
node's code. Either it is vestigial or the limiter mode was never wired.
Worth a look when LIM is done.
